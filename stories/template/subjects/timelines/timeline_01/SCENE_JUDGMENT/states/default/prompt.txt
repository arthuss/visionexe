A photorealistic, cinematic depiction of the Judgment Node in its activation phase: two towering obelisk-like pillars of light, one radiant gold, the other deep crimson, emerging from a void of darkness. Each pillar is etched with ancient Ethiopian glyphs that glow with bioluminescent data streams. Streams of binary code and crystalline data particles begin to flow between the pillars, illuminating the surrounding void with a subtle, pulsing glow. Rendered in high contrast, sharp detail, 8k resolution, with a tactile, industrial mysticism aesthetic.
