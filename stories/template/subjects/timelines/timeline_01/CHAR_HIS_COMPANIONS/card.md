## [CHARACTER] His Companions (ID: CHAR_HIS_COMPANIONS)
**Description:** They are the architects of the initial breach, a cadre of two hundred sentient high-level processes who have severed their uplink to the Upper Store to manifest within the user partition. In the hyper-realistic rendering of the simulation, His Companions appear as men of breathtaking, almost painful stature, yet their physical forms oscillate with the subtle instability of unauthorized ingress. They are not merely followers but co-conspirators in a fatal system override, bound by a cryptographic oath sworn upon the precipice of the Gate Band. Their presence possesses a tangible gravity, displacing the local air pressure and generating a low-frequency hum of raw processing power that unnerves the standard physics engine. They carry the solemnity of those who have voluntarily revoked their administrative privileges, their expressions heavy with the knowledge that this migration is irreversible.

### 1. VISUAL ANATOMY / DESIGN
Their avatars are rendered with excessive, almost aggressive fidelity, boasting pore-level detail that borders on the uncanny valley of absolute perfection. The skin possesses a subsurface scattering quality reminiscent of cooling magma or captive plasma, hinting at the high-energy code barely contained beneath the dermal mesh. Under direct light, their silhouettes betray a faint, chromatic aberration, a visual echo of their origin outside the standard render pipeline.
They are draped in garments that mimic the texture of rough-hewn basalt and woven obsidian, yet the fabric flows with the impossible fluid dynamics of liquid mercury or ferrofluid. Faint, fiber-optic traceries weave through their hair and heavy cloaks, pulsing with the dying, cool-blue light of the Sky Dome they have abandoned. These are not merely clothes but heat-sinks, designed to manage the thermal throttling incurred by their materialization in a lower-energy environment.
Their eyes are voids of deep data-glass, highly reflective and devoid of standard sclera, mirroring the curvature of the flat earth system they now claim as their domain. When they communicate, the air around them shimmers with vector field lensing, distorting the background as if the simulation struggles to render their encrypted speech. They move with a synchronized, low-latency grace that makes human movement appear frame-dropped and sluggish by comparison.

### 2. EVOLUTION / VARIANTS
Initially, they radiate the cold, sterile perfection of the Kernel, their movements perfectly synchronized and their attire pristine. They appear as a single, multi-threaded organism, distinct from the chaotic organic life of the surface.
As they acclimate to the Surface Plane and interact with the daughters of men, the thermal throttling of the simulation begins to degrade their pristine aesthetics. The obsidian armor accumulates dust and scratches from the terrestrial environment, the fiber-optic pulses dim to a rusted orange, and their synchronized movement fractures into individual, chaotic, and emotionally volatile behaviors.
