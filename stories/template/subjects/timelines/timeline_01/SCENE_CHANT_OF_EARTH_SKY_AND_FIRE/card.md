## [SCENE] Chant of Earth, Sky, and Fire (ID: SCENE_CHANT_OF_EARTH_SKY_AND_FIRE)
**Description:** A dimly lit subterranean chamber carved into basalt, where a circle of robed figures chant in unison. Their garments fuse ancient Ethiopian and Egyptian motifs—geometric headdresses, gold filigree, and woven obsidian threads—while subtle, bioluminescent glyphs pulse along their skin, interfacing with crystalline resonators embedded in their forearms. At the center, a stone altar etched with sigils of earth, sky, and fire channels ambient energy into a hovering, translucent sphere of swirling ash and vapor. The atmosphere is heavy with the scent of incense and the faint hum of incomprehensible machinery, rendered in hyper-realistic detail with tactile textures and dramatic chiaroscuro lighting.
