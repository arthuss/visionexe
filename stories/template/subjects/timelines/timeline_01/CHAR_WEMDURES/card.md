## [CHARACTER] Wemdures (ID: CHAR_WEMDURES)
**Description:** Wemdures is a towering sentinel of the Obsidian Temple, a fusion of basaltic stone and living crystal, standing 3.5 m tall. Its basaltic composite frame is reinforced with titanium alloy joints and covered in a translucent obsidian membrane that glows faintly with bio‑luminescent filaments. The face is a flat plane etched with ancient hieroglyphic glyphs; ocular arrays of opalescent crystal pulse amber data, and a central cranial node emits a steady amber glow. Wemdures wears a layered cuirass of hammered copper alloy, gold filigree, lapis lazuli inlay, and tempered glass plates. Embedded within its limbs is a living crystal lattice that channels energy, with bio‑luminescent filaments tracing the musculature. A tethered obsidian staff, its core etched with runic glyphs, completes the ensemble, casting a subtle glow on the stone floor.
