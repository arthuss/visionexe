## [SCENE] World to World (ID: SCENE_WORLD_TO_WORLD)
**Description:** A colossal, ancient-tech interdimensional gate rises from a barren desert, its massive stone base carved with Ethiopian and Egyptian hieroglyphs that glow with bioluminescent glyphs pulsing along the surface. The gate is composed of polished obsidian panels interlaced with crystalline conduits that refract light, and a central energy core emits a soft azure glow. Surrounding the gate are weathered obelisks and a runic tablet, all rendered in photorealistic detail, with dust motes dancing in shafts of harsh desert light, capturing a serious, cinematic atmosphere.
