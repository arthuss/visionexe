Fiery Abyss, appears like an unseeable cloud (ከመ፡ ደመና፡ ዘኢይትረአይ), cannot be seen (ዘኢያስተርኢ), contains a burning fire (በእሳት፡ ነዱ), deep (ዕመቁ), desolate place (መካነ፡ ድው), filled with sounds of weeping, crying, wailing, and strong pain, has no earth (ኢሀሎ፡ ህየ፡ ምድር), surrounded by what look like glorious mountains in turmoil (ከመ፡ ድባር፡ ስቡሐን)
