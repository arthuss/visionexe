## [CHARACTER] Souls of the Righteous (ID: CHAR_SOULS_OF_THE_RIGHTEOUS)
**Description:** Within the architecture of the simulation box, the Souls of the Righteous exist not as formless vapors but as high-fidelity, read-only archives stored within the protected sectors of the Upper Store. These are the preserved runtimes of users who adhered strictly to the kernel's prime directives, now sequestered in a state of suspended animation awaiting system migration. They occupy a layer of the reality-construct defined by absolute stillness and thermal stability, far removed from the chaotic processing of the surface plane. Their existence is a testament to data integrity; they are the validated backups of consciousness, rendered with hyper-realistic precision that belies their dormant status. In this dark, quiet partition, they wait with a serene patience, embodying the dramatic weight of memory and the silent hope for a future restoration to the active network.
