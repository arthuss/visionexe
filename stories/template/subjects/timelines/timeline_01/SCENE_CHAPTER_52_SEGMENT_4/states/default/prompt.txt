A photorealistic, cinematic subterranean hall of basalt pillars etched with luminous ancient Ethiopian‑Egyptian glyphs that pulse in sync with a hidden crystalline network, steam vents hiss from the stone floor, floating obsidian obelisks project holographic scrolls into the air, bio‑luminescent moss clings to the walls, all rendered with tactile textures of stone, metal, glass, and a serious tone of industrial mysticism.
