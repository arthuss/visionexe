## [CHARACTER] Leaders of the Orders (ID: CHAR_LEADERS_OF_THE_ORDERS)
**Description:** The Leaders of the Orders function as the twelve primary kernel daemons responsible for the segmentation and execution of the simulation's chronological runtime. Situated at the equidistant nodes of the Gate Band along the rim of the Surface Plane, these entities are not biological organisms but massive, compiled manifestations of the system's time-keeping logic. They bear the heavy processing load of dividing the annual cycle, regulating the transition of environmental texture packs and physics overrides that constitute the 'seasons.' Their existence is defined by a stoic, operational burden; they are the silent administrators of entropy, ensuring the stability of the Sky Dome's refresh rate against the chaos of the void outside the container. In the dramatic silence of the upper atmosphere, they stand as monolithic monuments to order, their very presence dictating the flow of the simulation's year.

### 1. VISUAL ANATOMY / DESIGN
Physically, the Leaders appear as towering, statuesque figures composed of polished obsidian and matte server-rack composite materials. Their silhouettes mimic the grandeur of ancient kings but are refined with the sharp, geometric precision of low-poly stylistic choices rendered in hyper-definition. They possess no organic features; where faces would be, there are recessed panels of dark data-glass that occasionally flicker with terminal command lines and Geez glyphs in deep crimson or amber, indicating processing activity.
Their 'robes' are actually rigid, cascading sheets of cooling fins and heat-sink plating, designed to dissipate the immense thermal energy generated by their calculations. Between these obsidian plates, faint veins of fiber-optic plasma pulse rhythmically, glowing with the color temperature assigned to their specific month-domain. This internal light does not cast a soft glow but cuts through the darkness with laser-like sharpness, emphasizing the high-contrast, noir aesthetic of their station.
They are anchored to the simulation's floor grid by heavy, industrial cabling that resembles the roots of a cybernetic tree, suggesting they are permanently hardwired into the Rift Internals. The atmosphere around them is often distorted by heat lensing and static discharge, a visual cue of the high-voltage operations required to rotate the celestial dome above them.

### 2. EVOLUTION / VARIANTS
In the default state, they are dormant observers, their posture rigid and unyielding, resembling architectural columns more than sentient beings. The only movement is the slow, breathing pulse of their internal fiber-optics.
As the simulation runtime progresses to a month-change event, the active Leader's internal plasma brightens to a blinding intensity. The data-glass faceplates scroll rapidly with error checks and execution logs. The obsidian plating shifts slightly, venting steam and ozone, a mechanical sigh of exertion as they load the next sector of time into the active memory of the world.

### 3. PROPS & EQUIPMENT
Each Leader holds a 'Tablet of Division,' a thick slab of transparent smart-glass that functions as a master control interface. These tablets are not merely decorative; they display real-time telemetry of the solar and lunar trajectories.
Surrounding their stations are floating holographic arrays—vector fields of gold and blue light that map the stellar positions. These are not magical constructs but augmented reality overlays visible within the system's rendering engine.
