## [CHARACTER] Phanuel (ID: CHAR_PHANUEL)
**Description:** Phanuel manifests as the Fourth Kernel Daemon within the simulation's administrative hierarchy, explicitly designated to manage the 'Repentance' subroutine—a critical rollback protocol for corrupted user-instances. In a runtime governed by strict input/output logic, he functions as the Auditor of State Recovery, overseeing the delicate process by which a fragmented entity can repair its checksums and qualify for migration to the persistent storage of the Upper Store. Unlike the militant firewalls or the patch-deploying healers, Phanuel represents the quiet, bureaucratic solemnity of data salvage. He exists in the high-latency zones of the simulation, the waiting rooms of the code, where he patiently reviews the error logs of those seeking to avoid deletion. His presence is heavy with the gravitational pull of a black hole, yet he offers the only valid egress key for entities that have violated their core programming.

These plates do not behave like fabric but like sliding tectonic shifts, reconfiguring around his frame to optimize cooling and data throughput. Etched into the obsidian are scrolling streams of Geez script—not mystical incantations, but live debugger output, glowing with a faint, clinical cyan luminescence that pulses with the rhythm of a heartbeat.
The dramatic visual language frames him in extreme close-up, utilizing a shallow depth of field to isolate his visage from the chaotic noise of the simulation. The lighting is always moody and high-contrast, utilizing 'God rays' that are actually projected volumetric light paths from the system's overhead grid.

The space behind his shoulders is distorted by intense heat shimmering and gravitational warping, creating a phantom plumage of refracted light. Fiber-optic bundles trail from his cranial ports like dreadlocks or a halo of wires, plugging directly into the atmospheric data-fog that permeates the simulation. When he moves, the air around him crackles with static discharge, the sound of a high-voltage server room amplified to a sacred hum.

### 2. EVOLUTION / VARIANTS
Throughout the narrative loop, Phanuel remains a constant, immutable variable—a fixed point of reference in a decaying system. His visual state does not degrade, reinforcing his role as the anchor of hope. However, the intensity of his internal processing manifests as an increase in thermal throttling; as the volume of 'repentance' requests spikes, the heat distortion around him becomes more violent, and the cyan glow of his debugger scripts brightens to a blinding white, casting harsh, dramatic shadows that carve his features into a mask of intense concentration.

He manipulates these hard-light structures with precise, pianist-like finger movements, literally untangling the 'spaghetti code' of a sinner's life to find the salvageable core.
Phanuel is often framed alongside the *Gateway Buffer*, a structural element of the simulation environment that resembles a confession booth constructed from server racks and privacy glass. This is his domain of operation, a space where the background noise of the world is dampened to absolute digital silence, allowing for the intimate, whispered exchange of authentication tokens between the fallen and the restorer.
