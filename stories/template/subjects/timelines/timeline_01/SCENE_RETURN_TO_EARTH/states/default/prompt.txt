A colossal ancient-tech spacecraft, its hull a lattice of obsidian glass and crystalline panels etched with Ethiopian and Egyptian hieroglyphs, descends through a storm‑laden sky onto a barren plateau. Bioluminescent veins pulse along the hull, casting an eerie glow over dust‑laden ground and towering stone monoliths. Cinematic lighting highlights the interplay of metal, stone, and light, evoking grounded realism and industrial mysticism in photorealistic detail.
