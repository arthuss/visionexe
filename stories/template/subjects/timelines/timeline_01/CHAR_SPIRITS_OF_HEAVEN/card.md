## [CHARACTER] Spirits of Heaven (ID: CHAR_SPIRITS_OF_HEAVEN)
**Description:** The Spirits of Heaven are the incarnate logic of the Upper Store, distinct from the corrupted, heavy executables that roam the surface plane. They are the system’s original source code, compiled into semi-translucent, refractive hulls of data-glass and obsidian. Where the 'Evil Spirits' are glitches—heavy, hungry, and bound to the thermal throttle of the earth—these entities operate at the speed of light, existing within the cool, superconductive vacuum of the simulation's zenith. They do not bleed; they leak plasma. They do not speak; they transmit high-bandwidth packets of authorized truth. To the human observer, they appear not as biological kin, but as beautiful, terrifying inevitabilities of the system's architecture, observing the runtime with the detached precision of automated daemons.

### 1. VISUAL ANATOMY / DESIGN
Their physical forms are defined by refractive indices rather than opacity, resembling silhouettes carved from dielectric mirrors and smart glass. They possess no organic porosity or surface imperfections; their 'skin' is a seamless, friction-less barrier that repels the dust of the lower atmosphere.
Internally, a nervous system of glowing fiber-optic filaments replaces veins, pulsing with the rhythmic clock speed of the central kernel. These filaments carry golden instruction sets that illuminate their limbs from within, casting a harsh, cinematic glow on the obsidian ground beneath them.
Their movement violates the standard physics of the surface layer. They do not walk so much as update their position coordinates, gliding with the unnatural smoothness of a high-framerate rendering. Their presence generates local vector-field lensing, warping the air around them as if the simulation struggles to render their high-fidelity geometry alongside the low-poly background.

### 2. EVOLUTION / VARIANTS
As static constants of the simulation's kernel, they do not age or degrade. Their visual state remains pristine, a 'Default' condition of optimal performance that contrasts sharply with the decaying textures of the mortal world.
However, their luminosity fluctuates based on system load. In moments of high processing—judgment or revelation—their internal fiber optics flare to blinding intensity, turning their glass bodies into pillars of solid white data.

### 3. PROPS & EQUIPMENT

Some carry tablets of transparent quartz that scroll with infinite lines of code, representing the immutable laws of the heavenly luminaries and the recorded sins of the surface.
