The obelisks—six in total—rise like sentinels, each 12 meters tall, tapering to a pointed apex. The central engine sits on a raised dais, its brass casing etched with intricate filigree.
*   **Face/Sensors:** The engine’s surface is studded with a lattice of translucent crystal lenses that act as sensors, capturing ambient vibrations and translating them into light patterns. The obelisks feature embedded copper conduits that glow faintly, indicating power flow.
*   **Clothing/Armor:** The chamber’s environment itself serves as armor: the basalt walls are reinforced with a hidden lattice of carbon‑fiber strands, invisible to the eye but providing structural integrity. The obelisks are coated in a thin layer of nano‑ceramic that protects against corrosion and enhances conductivity.
*   **Key Features:** 1) Obelisks of obsidian and copper with crystalline cores. 2) Central brass engine with crystal sensor lattice. 3) Luminous glyphs embedded in basalt walls. 4) Bio‑luminescent mist swirling in the air. 5) Subtle play of light on polished surfaces, creating a sense of depth and mysticism.
