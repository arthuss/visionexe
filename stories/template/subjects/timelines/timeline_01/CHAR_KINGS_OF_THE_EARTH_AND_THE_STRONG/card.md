## [CHARACTER] Kings of the earth and the Strong (ID: CHAR_KINGS_OF_THE_EARTH_AND_THE_STRONG)
**Description:** Within the bounded system of the simulation, the Kings of the Earth and the Strong function as high-priority sub-processes who have monopolized the surface plane's resource allocation. They are not merely wealthy; they are administrators of the local runtime, adorned in data-glass raiment that signifies their read-write access to the persistent storage layers (the 'dry ground'). They operate under the delusion that their user privileges are absolute, denying the existence of the Root Admin (the Lord of Spirits) and the authentication protocols of the Anointed. Their existence is one of excessive bandwidth consumption and latency-free luxury, unaware that they are operating within a sandbox scheduled for a hard reset.

### 1. VISUAL ANATOMY / DESIGN
In their prime execution state, these entities appear as hyper-realistic, high-polygon avatars, projected with zero aliasing and perfect ray-traced reflections. Their attire suggests a fusion of ancient royalty and futuristic hardware: robes woven from active fiber-optic cabling that pulses with golden data streams, and chest plates of polished obsidian that act as localized heat sinks. Their skin possesses a subsurface scattering quality that mimics organic life to an uncanny degree, yet their eyes often betray the cold logic of a merciless algorithm, glowing with the faint blue of system idle lights.
The environment they inhabit reflects their status: stark, vertical architectures of chrome and glass, situated high above the noise floor of the common simulation. They are often framed in slow, cinematic camera movements that emphasize their scale relative to the 'surface plane' they dominate. The lighting around them is artificial and controlled—high-contrast studio setups that eliminate unsightly shadows, reinforcing the illusion of their flawlessness.
As the judgment sequence initiates, the visual fidelity of these avatars begins to degrade. The 'fire' that consumes them is rendered not as organic flame, but as a violent thermal throttling event—plasma-like artifacts and screen-tearing that rip through their meticulously rendered meshes. They become heavy, their optimized code bloating into dead weight ('lead'), dragging them down into the 'water'—the dark, cooling reservoirs of the system's waste collection, where they sink without buoyancy, their privileges revoked.

### 2. EVOLUTION / VARIANTS
Initially, they stand rigid and imposing, their movements smooth and interpolated at high frame rates, projecting an aura of unassailable stability. They look down from their high towers, their faces masks of arrogant serenity.
Upon the execution of the judgment kernel, this stability fractures. Their movements become jittery, suffering from lag and packet loss. The transition is dramatic: the golden light of their fiber-optics turns to a warning red, then extinguishes. Their forms, once solid, begin to delaminate like burning grass—layers of texture stripping away to reveal the wireframe void beneath—before they ultimately collapse into heavy, inert matter that plunges into the abyss of the system's purge buffers.
