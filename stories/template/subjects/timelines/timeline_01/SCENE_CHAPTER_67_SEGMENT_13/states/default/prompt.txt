A colossal 3‑meter obsidian sphere, the Obsidian Resonance Core, sits at the heart of a dimly lit industrial cavern. Its surface is etched with a fusion of ancient Ethiopian and Egyptian glyphs that are barely visible, the copper and bronze conduits are inert, and the sphere emits no glow or hum. Photorealistic, tactile textures, cinematic lighting, realistic shadows, high detail.
