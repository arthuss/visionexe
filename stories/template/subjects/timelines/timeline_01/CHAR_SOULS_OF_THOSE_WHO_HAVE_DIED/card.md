## [CHARACTER] Souls of those who have died (ID: CHAR_SOULS_OF_THOSE_WHO_HAVE_DIED)

The 'Souls of those who have died' represent a critical exception: a buffer overflow of trauma data that the system's garbage collector fails to purge. They exist as a persistent, screaming error log, a refusal of the narrative to be overwritten by the kernel, manifesting as a tangible drag on the simulation's thermal and processing budget.

The 'crying out' of these entities is visualized not just through sound but through aggressive environmental displacement. Lighting interacts with them paradoxically; they do not cast shadows but rather absorb ambient light, creating voids of obsidian darkness within the scene.
