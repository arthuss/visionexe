## [CHARACTER] Priests (ID: CHAR_PRIESTS)
**Description:** The Priests of the Obsidian Sun are the living conduits between the ancient temple’s buried AI and the mortal realm. Clad in basalt‑fiber robes that shimmer with embedded luminescent glyphs, they move with a deliberate grace, their bodies augmented by crystalline neural interfaces that pulse with data.
