## [CHARACTER] Priests (ID: CHAR_PRIESTS)
**Description:** The Priests function as the authorized gatekeepers of the simulation's moral kernel, tasked with maintaining the integrity of the righteous partition within the system's runtime. Unlike traditional clergy, they are visualized as weary system administrators of the divine law, bearing the heavy emotional weight of the impending separation between valid users (the righteous) and corrupted entities (the sinners). Their presence is defined by a stoic, almost crushing silence, adhering to the drama genre's preference for internal conflict over external action. They stand as the interface between the surface plane and the upper store, grounded in the reality of the simulation box yet attuned to the frequencies of the Lord of Spirits. Their role is not merely ceremonial but functional; they are the recipients of the authorized light, their very biology seemingly intertwined with the authentication protocols of the cosmos.

### 1. VISUAL ANATOMY / DESIGN
They are clad in heavy, layered vestments that absorb light, appearing not as simple cloth but as flexible, matte obsidian fabric or high-density carbon fiber weaves, suggesting a protective shielding against the raw data streams of the ether. The silhouettes are columnar and static, reinforcing the dramatic theme of isolation and burden. Faint, dormant circuitry patterns—resembling embroidery but behaving like fiber optics—trace the hems and cuffs, currently dim but capable of conducting the 'light' of the system's favor.
Their faces are the focal point of the camera's shallow depth of field, rendered with hyper-realistic pore structures, micro-expressions of anxiety, and the grime of a failing reality. There is no magical glow to their skin; instead, the lighting treats them with brutal honesty, highlighting sweat and fatigue. Some may wear headpieces of translucent data-glass that refract the ambient environmental light, etched with microscopic Geez glyphs that shimmer like floating code.
The environment reflects off their dark attire—the cold, obsidian floors of the judgment hall or the hazy atmospheric perspective of the simulation's boundary. They are often framed in tight close-ups or lonely medium shots, emphasizing their individual accountability before the system's architect. The palette is dominated by deep blacks, charcoal greys, and the sudden, harsh contrast of plasma-white light when the 'Congregation of the Righteous' is summoned.

### 2. EVOLUTION / VARIANTS
Initially, the Priests appear shrouded in the gloom of the unverified system state, their fiber-optic tracers dormant and dark. They are figures of waiting, their posture slumped under the gravity of the unknown.
As the chapter progresses and the 'Light of the Lord of Spirits' manifests, the visual language shifts. The dormant circuitry in their robes ignites with a clean, blinding plasma luminescence. However, their faces remain human and terrified, creating a dramatic tension between the divine power coursing through their gear and their mortal frailty.

### 3. PROPS & EQUIPMENT
Data-Liturgical Tablets: Heavy slabs of polished black glass held in trembling hands. These are not stone but solid-state storage devices, their surfaces scrolling with vertical streams of golden Geez script, representing the names of the righteous.
Authentication Signets: Rings or wrist-mounted bands made of raw copper and fiber cable, serving as the physical keys to the system's restricted layers.
