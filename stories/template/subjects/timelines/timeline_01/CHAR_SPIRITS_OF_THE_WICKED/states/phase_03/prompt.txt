A wide, atmospheric shot of the Desolate Place, a flat, endless plane of obsidian glass under a sky of static noise. Scattered across the horizon are the fading remnants of the Wicked—glowing, translucent silhouettes that flicker in and out of existence. They are no longer fully defined human shapes but wandering columns of corrupted code and ember-like data particles.
