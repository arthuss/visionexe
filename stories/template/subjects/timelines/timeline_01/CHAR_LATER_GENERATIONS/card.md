## [CHARACTER] Later generations (ID: CHAR_LATER_GENERATIONS)
**Description:** The 'Later Generations' are not merely biological descendants but the optimized future iterations of the human kernel, scheduled for deployment in subsequent runtime cycles of the flat-earth simulation. Within the bounded system box, they exist as a heavy, expectant presence—the compiled target of the current processing struggle. They represent the system's intended stability state, entities programmed to execute the 'righteousness' protocol without the glitch-prone variances or corruption artifacts of the Watcher-infected builds. They are the silent, watchful audience for whom the Enoch-Voyager compiles his intricate log files, appearing in the narrative space as spectral possibilities, bathed in the soft, promising glow of a system update that has not yet fully propagated across the surface plane.

### 1. VISUAL ANATOMY / DESIGN
Visually, these entities appear with absolute mesh integrity, lacking the jitter, temporal noise, or texture-popping common to the current, degraded generation. Their skin possesses the flawless subsurface scattering of a high-budget render, yet retains a cool, digital luminescence suggestive of the upper store's ambient lighting. They are often framed in static, symmetrical compositions that emphasize their stability, captured with a shallow depth of field that isolates them from the chaotic background noise of the uncorrected world.
Their attire eschews the ragged, procedurally generated look of the desert wanderers for streamlined, fiber-optic weaves that imply advanced resource allocation and optimized geometry. The materials are hyper-realistic, mimicking organic linen and silk but with a subtle, underlying data-grid pattern visible only under the high-contrast flare of the simulation's sun. They carry the visual weight of 'peace'—balanced, uncluttered silhouettes rendered in harmonious, desaturated tones that stand in stark contrast to the harsh plasma burns of the corrupted actors.
When addressed by the narrative voice, they are rarely depicted in kinetic action. Instead, the camera treats them with the reverence of a slow cinema drama—long, unbroken takes that linger on their expectant faces and calm postures. The lighting around them is softer, approximating the 'golden hour' of a simulated sunrise, a visual metaphor for the dawn of a corrected system state where the code of righteousness runs without exception.

### 2. EVOLUTION / VARIANTS
Initially, they manifest as abstract concepts referenced in the metadata of Enoch's manual, visible only as translucent overlays or reflections in the data-glass surfaces of the Rift Internals. They are the ghosts of the future, watching the current drama unfold with solemn anticipation, their forms flickering slightly as if the simulation has not yet allocated them full memory.
By the time of the final admonitions in Chapter 92, they solidify into distinct, high-fidelity entities. The fog of probability lifts to reveal them as the inevitable result of the Voyager's work. They stand ready to inherit the simulation box, their very presence validating the accumulation of data and the rejection of the Watchers' viral influence.

### 3. PROPS & EQUIPMENT

These devices emit a steady, calming blue light that illuminates their faces, serving as the only light source in the dark, undefined spaces they occupy.
Surrounding them are faint, holographic projections of 'The Plant of Righteousness,' rendered as perfect, fractal vegetation algorithms that drift like 3D screensavers. These distinct visual markers symbolize the successful execution of the long-term biological program, contrasting with the withered, arid assets of the current simulation cycle.
