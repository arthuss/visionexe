## [CHARACTER] Sinners (ID: CHAR_SINNERS)
**Description:** The Sinners represent the corrupted user base of the simulation, a collective of entities who have exploited the runtime environment to hoard system resources and bypass the kernel's ethical protocols. They are the Lawless, the architects of a destabilized reality who have rewritten the surface plane's local code to favor their own accumulation of bandwidth and high-fidelity assets. Far from being chaotic, they are coldly calculated in their oppression, presenting themselves with the polished veneer of system administrators while denying the authority of the Prime Architect. Their existence is a paradox of high-resolution luxury built upon a decaying codebase; they feast on digital ambrosia and adorn themselves in data-glass finery, oblivious that their unchecked consumption is triggering a thermal throttling event that will inevitably lead to their deletion. They are the 'hard of heart,' effectively read-only memory blocks that refuse to accept the update patches sent by the Watchers.

### 1. VISUAL ANATOMY / DESIGN
In the flourishing phase, the Sinners are visually distinct through their hyper-realistic rendering, contrasting sharply with the lower-fidelity existence of the righteous. Their skin possesses a flawless, almost synthetic perfection, exhibiting calculated subsurface scattering that speaks of excessive resource allocation. They are draped in garments of woven fiber-optics and liquid obsidian, materials that do not merely reflect light but seem to absorb and repurpose it. They inhabit the upper registers of the simulation box, dwelling in architectural structures of glass and chrome that pierce the lower cloud layers, physically elevating them above the common runtime.
Their movements are characterized by a cinematic smoothness, a high-frame-rate fluidity that implies a lack of friction or resistance from the world physics. However, this perfection is a façade. Close-ups reveal a coldness in the eyes—a lack of connection to the central network, a void where empathy should reside. The 'great and hard words' they speak materialize as dissonant glyphs in the air, jagged vectors of corrupted data that disturb the atmosphere around them. They hold tablets of dark glass, inscribed with the 'falsehoods' of their own making, rewriting the logs of the simulation to justify their theft.
As judgment initiates, the visual stability of the Sinners begins to degrade. The hyper-realism fractures. We see compression artifacts manifesting on their fine clothing; their movements begin to drop frames, stuttering in a tragic loop of denial. The warm, golden lighting that once bathed their banquets shifts to a harsh, diagnostic red, exposing the wireframes beneath their skin. The 'wealth' they clung to—glowing orbs of raw compute and crystallized data—begins to evaporate, leaking into the atmosphere as meaningless noise, leaving them grasping at null pointers.

### 2. EVOLUTION / VARIANTS
The arc of the Sinners is a descent from absolute control to total system rejection. Initially, they are the masters of the frame, occupying the center with static, symmetrical compositions that emphasize their dominance and stability. They are filmed with wide lenses that exaggerate their stature against the curvature of the dome. The camera lingers on the textures of their excess—the heavy rings, the fine fabrics—establishing the weight of their materialism.
As the narrative shifts to judgment, the cinematography destabilizes. The static frames are replaced by handheld, uneasy motion, reflecting the tremor in the simulation's foundation. The focus becomes shallow, isolating individual Sinners as their collective power dissolves. We witness the 'thermal throttling' of their avatars—sweat that looks like melting silicon, skin that greys into untextured clay. The final phase is a descent into the 'null void,' where they are stripped of all rendering, reduced to silhouette and shadow, screaming into a darkness that offers no bandwidth for their pleas.

### 3. PROPS & EQUIPMENT
They carry 'Tablets of Falsehood,' slim panes of black glass that emit a pale, sickly light, displaying the rewritten code of the simulation which they use to enforce their unjust laws. These devices are sleek, finger-print magnets that serve as symbols of their administrative overreach.
Their 'Wealth' is depicted not just as gold, but as containers of raw luminance—sealed vials of plasma and compressed light that hum with the energy stolen from the system's grid. These artifacts are heavy, burdening their owners even as they display them with pride.
