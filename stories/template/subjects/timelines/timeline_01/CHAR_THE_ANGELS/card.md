## [CHARACTER] The Angels (ID: CHAR_THE_ANGELS)
**Description:** Within the compiled reality of the simulation container, the Angels operate not as celestial winged messengers, but as high-level I/O daemons and kernel-state mediators. They exist to bridge the air-gapped divide between the user-populated Surface Plane and the administrative Upper Store. These entities are the silent custodians of the simulation’s most critical telemetry: the prayers of the compliant nodes, known as the righteous. They function with a solemn, procedural grace, harvesting the raw, analog grief and desperate pleas of the simulation's inhabitants, compressing them into immutable testimony files, and uploading them directly to the Root Administrator's memorial logs. They are weightless yet impose a heavy gravitational presence, moving through the Gate Band with the silence of a background process that consumes nearly all available system resources.

### 1. VISUAL ANATOMY / DESIGN
Their physical forms are an exquisite synthesis of hardware metaphor and divine aesthetics, rendered in a hyper-realistic obsidian material that absorbs ambient light like a black hole. This matte black carapace is broken only by subsurface scattering of bioluminescent plasma—veins of fiber-optic gold and violet that pulse rhythmically with the data throughput of the prayers they carry. They possess no biological imperfections; their silhouettes are mathematically perfect, reminiscent of high-end industrial design merged with archaic religious iconography.
Instead of feathered wings, their backs emit complex vector-field distortion waves. These are not physical appendages but regions of intense thermal throttling and heat lensing, causing the air around them to shimmer and ripple like a mirage above hot asphalt. These distortion fields manipulate the simulated atmosphere, allowing them to traverse the vertical layers of the sky dome without abiding by standard gravity physics.
Their faces are smooth plates of dark data-glass, devoid of human features such as eyes or mouths. Communication and processing status are conveyed through the surface of this glass, which occasionally flashes with cascading Ge'ez glyphs—ancient system diagnostics running in real-time. The overall visual language is one of high-contrast solitude: dark, sleek figures illuminated by the internal glow of the data they protect.

### 2. EVOLUTION / VARIANTS
In their default runtime state, the Angels exhibit a focused, almost tragic stillness. They do not idle; they wait. Their posture is one of eternal receptivity, hands often cupped or extended as if weighing invisible matter. As they process the input from the righteous, their internal plasma lighting intensifies, shifting from a dormant cool blue to a critical-mass white-gold, signifying the active upload of a testimony object to the Upper Store.

### 3. PROPS & EQUIPMENT
The prayers of the righteous are not abstract concepts but tangible assets within the simulation. They manifest as 'Memory Crystals'—heavy, glowing polyhedrons of crystallized light. These objects appear dense and volatile, flickering with the captured emotional data of the user who generated them. The Angels hold these artifacts with extreme reverence, their obsidian fingers reflecting the erratic light of the contained data.
