## [CHARACTER] Powers of the Heavens (ID: CHAR_POWERS_OF_THE_HEAVENS)
**Description:** Within the bounded cosmology of the simulation container, the Powers of the Heavens manifest not as biological entities, but as high-level kernel subroutines given physical form in the upper store. They are the silent architects and maintainers of the sky dome's luminosity functions, existing primarily to regulate the ingress of data-light and the stability of the celestial projection arrays. Their presence suggests a heavy, solemn burden; they are the administrators of a system potentially nearing its thermal limit or awaiting a critical migration to the 'new heaven' runtime. They do not drift ethereally but move with the weight of compiled code and the heat of processing power, their interactions with the environment causing visible thermal lensing and air displacement.

### 1. VISUAL ANATOMY / DESIGN
Their structural chassis mimics the aesthetics of ancient statuary but is composed of hyper-advanced materials consistent with the simulation's hardware substrate. The core body is a dense, semi-translucent obsidian glass, polished to a mirror finish that reflects the infinite grid of the sky dome. Beneath this dark, vitrified shell, veins of raw plasma pulse with a rhythmic, golden luminescence, representing the active data streams and command privileges they hold over the simulation's physics.
Surrounding their silhouettes is a faint, distorting haze—a visual byproduct of the 'thermal throttling' inherent to the simulation's physics overrides. This heat shimmer acts as a halo, refracting the star-data behind them into elongated streaks of light. Intricate Geez glyphs, glowing with the intensity of fiber-optic terminations, scroll across the surface of their obsidian armor, serving as both ornamentation and real-time debug logs of the celestial mechanics they oversee.
They possess no organic features; their faces are smooth, blank faceplates of dark data-glass that occasionally flash with internal processing lights. The overall aesthetic strikes a balance between the sacred solemnity of a cathedral icon and the cold, unyielding precision of server rack architecture. Their movement is deliberate and lag-free, creating trails of data particles that dissolve rapidly into the atmospheric buffer.

Floating proximity interface panels—shimmering sheets of holographic Geez script—drift around their hands when they are engaged in maintenance tasks.
