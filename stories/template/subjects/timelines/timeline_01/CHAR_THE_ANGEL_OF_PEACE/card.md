## [CHARACTER] The Angel of Peace (ID: CHAR_THE_ANGEL_OF_PEACE)
**Description:** The Angel of Peace manifests as a high-fidelity system administrator within the simulation's runtime environment, a specialized daemon designed for user guidance and protocol stabilization. Unlike the aggressive sentinel logic of other watchers, this entity radiates a profound, calculated equilibrium, functioning as the primary interface between the observer (the Voyager) and the raw data constructs of the simulation's deep storage—specifically the resource heaps manifested as mountains of metal. The entity's presence dampens local volatility, enforcing a localized physics override that allows for calm, didactic interaction amidst the potential chaos of the kernel's manifestation. It is an avatar of absolute clarity, appearing not merely as a celestial being, but as the personification of a perfectly optimized operating system at rest, guiding the user through the impending resource purge where the metallic mountains will liquefy like corrupted sectors during a format.

### 1. VISUAL ANATOMY / DESIGN
The entity's physical form suggests a hyper-realistic sculpture of translucent alabaster and matte polycarbonate, skin boasting an impossible sub-surface scattering that mimics the warmth of life but retains the coolness of a machine. The facial features are perfectly symmetrical, evocative of classical statuary but rendered with the uncanny precision of a 16K texture map. The eyes are not biological; they are twin spheres of polished obsidian, occasionally overlaid with faint, scrolling Geez glyphs in a cyan phosphor hue when accessing the system's backend data to explain the environment.
Vestments drape over the frame with the heavy, liquid physics of a simulation running at maximum dampening. The robes are composed of a material resembling woven fiber-optic cabling and dark ballistic silk, shifting subtly between deep indigo and void-black. There are no seams, only procedural folds that adjust to the entity's minimal movements. Around the neck and shoulders, a collar of floating data-glass shards forms a fragmented halo, refracting the ambient light of the simulation's sky-dome into spectral warnings.
Movement is deliberately slow and cinematic, lacking the micro-jitters of biological fatigue. Every gesture is a keyframe-perfect transition, designed to lower the cognitive load on the observer. The entity does not walk so much as glide just millimeters above the surface plane, disturbing the dust of the simulation floor with a subtle electrostatic repulsion field rather than physical impact.
