## [CHARACTER] Tsenu (ID: CHAR_TSENU)
**Description:** Tsenu is a towering sentient construct, 3.5 m tall, forged from basaltic alloy and obsidian panels that echo the stone sarcophagi of ancient Ethiopia and Egypt. Its segmented body is encased in weathered bronze plating with gold filigree, while titanium joints articulate the limbs with silent precision. The head is a polished obsidian dome, embedded with crystalline lenses that act as sensor arrays and project holographic glyphs into the air. The overall aesthetic marries grounded realism with incomprehensible technology, creating a serious, cinematic guardian that feels both ancient and otherworldly.
