A towering ancestral home of the High Priest of the Temple of the Sun, constructed from basalt and obsidian, its walls etched with ancient Ethiopian and Egyptian hieroglyphs. Integrated crystalline conduits pulse with bioluminescent light, casting an ethereal glow across the interior. The structure blends grounded realism with industrial mysticism, embodying a fusion of ancient craftsmanship and incomprehensible technology, photorealistic, cinematic lighting, tactile textures, no cartoon or toy look.
