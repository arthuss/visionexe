CONCEPT_DEFINITION: PROTOCOL_MIGRATION_V2
REVISION: 2.0 (STRICT_MECHANISM)

1. BIO-INJECTION (THE UPDATE):
The "Mitet" (Change) is a `MATERIAL_INSTANCE_OVERWRITE`. The biological epidermis of the Elect is not merely turned to marble, but replaced by `CERAMIC_HEAT_SHIELDING` (Matte White, Roughness 0.1) to withstand the high-energy latency of the "Light of Days" (Root Access). Subdermal vascular systems are purged of hemoglobin and replaced with `FIBER_OPTIC_BUS_LINES` carrying high-frequency data (Cyan/Gold). The "Glory" is an AR Overlay: a rotating, complex `USER_ID_HEADER` floating above the cranium, signaling Verified Admin Status.

2. MESH-BREAK (THE ERROR):
The Sinners suffer from `POLYGON_DECIMATION`. As "unrighteousness" (corrupt data) piles up, their Level-of-Detail (LOD) crashes. They degrade from High-Poly humanoids to Low-Poly abstractions, then to chaotic wireframes. Their textures unwrap in real-time (`UV_UNWRAP_FAIL`), exposing raw flesh-tone maps and checkerboard error shaders. The "Works of their hands" manifest as `LOG_FILES`: heavy, floating black text strings of hexadecimal error codes that tether them to the ground like chains.

3. PACING-STRATEGIE:
`BUFFER_UNDERRUN_PACING`.
The Holy sequences run at a locked, perfect framerate (simulation of stability).
The Sinner sequences introduce `FRAME_DROPPING` and `DATAMOSHING` artifacts. The edit cuts are not rhythmic music video cuts, but "System Interrupts"—black screens for 2 frames, jarring jumps in continuity, simulating a struggling processor.

4. AUDIO-DESIGN:
No choirs. The "Name of the Lord of Spirits" is a `CRYPTOGRAPHIC_HANDSHAKE`. It sounds like a 56k modem dial-up sequence slowed down by 8000%, transforming it into a massive, tectonic grinding noise that resolves into a pure, mathematical chord.
Background: The hum of server cooling fans (The Presence) and the wet tearing sound of mesh separating (The Deletion).

5. TRANSITIONS:
`HEX_GRID_WIPE`. The reality doesn't dissolve; it is re-indexed. A scanning line (Green/White) passes over objects. Behind the line, the asset is updated (Holy) or removed (Sinner).

6. FRAMEWORK & TIMING:
Total Duration: Approx. 75 Seconds.
Clip Count: 15 Clips.
Structure:
- 00-15s: The Migration Request (Seg 1: The Change)
- 15-35s: The Log Dump (Seg 2: Unrighteousness piled up)
- 35-50s: The Key Verification (Seg 3: Saved by the Name)
- 50-65s: The Runtime Error (Seg 4: Judgment)
- 65-75s: The Formatting (Seg 5: No Mercy)

7. DIALOG-CHECK:
Minimal. The "Speaker" (Uriel/System Voice) announces the State Switch.
Shot-Reverse-Shot is required for the "Showing to others" (Seg 2). We need to see the Sinners looking at the Holy, then the Holy looking back stoically.

8. SPATIAL CONTEXT (ARRIVAL):
Location: `SERVER_NODE_ZION_PRIME`.
Arrival: No movement. They are initialized in place. The camera does a "Boot Sequence" zoom—starting from a satellite view (orthographic) and snapping instantly to a ground-level view of the grid.
