CONCEPT_DEFINITION: PROTOCOL_LUMEN_INJECTION

1. BIO-INJECTION (THE CHANGE):
The "Light of Days" manifests as a complete texture-map overwrite. The epidermis of the Holy Subjects transitions from biological tissue to `TRANSLUCENT_PARIAN_MARBLE`. Subdermal veins are purged of blood and refilled with `LIQUID_GOLD_PHOTONICS` (6000K emissivity). The "Glory" is a visible halo-interface: a floating, rotating ring of Ge'ez glyphs above the cranium, signifying Root Access.

2. MESH-BREAK (THE AFFLICTION):
For the Sinners, the "treasuring up of evil" is visualized as `GEOMETRY_CACHE_OVERFLOW`. Their physical mesh becomes unstable. Vertices vibrate violently (Z-fighting). Textures flicker between "Flesh" and "Raw Meat". The "Works of their hands" manifest as heavy, black obsidian chains growing out of their forearms, weighing down the mesh until it stretches and tears.

3. PACING-STRATEGIE:
Dual-Rate Rendering.
The Holy are rendered at a perceived 120fps: smooth, fluid, slow-motion grace.
The Sinners are rendered at a perceived 12fps: stuttering, jarring, missing frames.
The edit cuts sharply between these two frame rates to induce visceral discomfort in the viewer.

4. AUDIO-DESIGN:
The "Name of the Lord of Spirits" is not a choir, but a `RESONANT_FREQUENCY_SWEEP`. It starts as a deep, chest-rattling sub-bass (30Hz) and sweeps up to a piercing crystalline sine wave (4000Hz).
Background ambience: The hum of a massive, high-voltage transformer (The Presence) overlaid with the sound of tearing metal (The Sinners' mesh breaking).

5. TRANSITIONS:
`VOXEL_DISSOLVE`. The transition from the "Old State" to the "New State" isn't a fade. The reality decomposes into 1cm cubes for a split second, then reassembles in the upgraded configuration.

6. FRAMEWORK & TIMING:
Total Duration: 60 Seconds.
Clip Count: 14 Clips.
Structure:
- 00-15s: The Injection (Segment 1)
- 15-35s: The Witnessing & Glitch (Segment 2 & 3)
- 35-50s: The Judgment (Segment 4)
- 50-60s: The Deletion (Segment 5)

7. DIALOG-CHECK:
Yes. The "Speaker" (System Voice/Uriel) delivers the verdict in Segment 4 & 5.
Technique: No lip-sync. The voice resonates from the environment itself. The Speaker's face is a static, glowing mask.

8. SPATIAL CONTEXT:
Location: `BUFFER_ZONE_ZERO`. An infinite plane of polished black basalt.
Sky: No stars. A ceiling of rolling, heavy gray data-clouds.
Arrival: The subjects are already present, arranged in a grid formation. The camera tracks through the grid.
