2. MESH-BREAK
   - **Mechanism:** 'RENDER_DISTANCE_CULLING'.
   - **Manifestation:** The "Ends of the Earth" are not a physical cliff but a degradation of the simulation fidelity at the map limits.

   - **Camera:** Mechanical gimbals. Perfect mathematical pans. No handheld shake. The camera moves like a scanner head.

   - **Visual:** The screen briefly pixelates (macro-blocking) or tears horizontally (V-Sync failure) when switching compass directions. A "Loading..." spinner or command line text flashes for 1 frame between sectors.
