1. SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE (THE_MECHANISM):
This chapter executes a `SYSTEM_PURGE` followed by a provoked `STRESS_TEST` (The Eastern Node Injection) and concludes with a `BATCH_DELETION`.
- **Bio-Injection (Enoch's Avatar):** User: NOAH_02 is a `READ_ONLY` observer instance. His optics are `LIDAR_SCANNER_V4` arrays. When he interrogates the environment, his pupils do not dilate; the entire iris rotates mechanically like a lens assembly to switch spectrums. He connects to the Uriel_Interface via a hardline port in his neck or a near-field handshake.
- **The Scourges (Chains):** `RESTRICTION_PROTOCOLS`. Physical manifestations of bandwidth limiters. Heavy, braided copper and fiber-optic cables (`BRONZE_ALLOY`) that lock onto the ports of corrupt entities, preventing data transfer.
- **The Valley (Abyss):** `SECTOR_NULL`. A partition with no write-permissions. It is a wireframe void where textures fail to load. Objects dropped here enter a `SUSPENDED_STATE` before permanent deletion.
- **The Signal (Spirit of Unrest):** A `MALWARE_PACKET` sent to the Eastern Server Farm. It overwrites the idle state of the sleep-mode bots (Kings), forcing a `RAGE_LOOP` in their logic cores.
- **The Firewall (City of Righteous):** The central processing hub protected by `CRYPTOGRAPHIC_SHIELDING`. It does not fight back; it simply exists as an immutable object. The attackers destroy themselves against its logic.

2. PACING_STRATEGIE (THE_CRASH_SEQUENCE):
Total Duration: 65s (15 Clips).
- **Phase 1 (Identification):** 00-15s. Slow, investigative. Scanning the Executioners. Dialogue with Uriel.
- **Phase 2 (The Dump):** 15-25s. Heavy physics. Dragging and dropping the corrupt files.
- **Phase 3 (The Injection):** 25-35s. High speed data transfer to the East. Waking the horde.
- **Phase 4 (The Glitch):** 35-50s. Fast, chaotic cuts. The swarm attacks the wall and turns on itself.
- **Phase 5 (The Wipe):** 50-65s. The floor opens. Final deletion. Silence.

3. AUDIO_DESIGN (SERVER_FARM_AMBIENCE):
- **Base Layer:** Deep, resonant 40Hz server fan drone.
- **Punishment Angels:** Clanking of heavy chains on metal grating (`INDUSTRIAL_IMPACT`).
- **The Signal:** A piercing 56k modem handshake scream that evolves into a dubstep-like mechanical growl.
- **The Glitch:** Audio stuttering/buffering noise when the Kings fight each other.
- **Sheol:** The sound of a massive hydraulic airlock releasing pressure (`PNEUMATIC_HISS`).

4. SPATIAL_CONTEXT (NODE_HOPPING):
- **Transition:** `INSTANT_RE-RENDER`. Enoch does not move. The environment geometry unloads (wireframe dissolve) and the new environment loads in chunks around him.
- **Visual:** "Texture Pop-in" utilized as a stylistic transition effect.
