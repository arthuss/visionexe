TECHNICAL MECHANICS: CHAPTER 97 - THE LIQUIDITY AUDIT

1. BIO-INJECTION (THE PARALLAX):
Henoch's optical cortex is overridden by the `AUDIT_VISUALIZER_V4`. This is not a biological mutation but a temporary firmware injection via the optic nerve. He perceives "Wealth" not as material, but as `HEAP_ALLOCATION_BLOCKS`. When the audit begins, valid blocks remain stable (Green wireframe overlay); illicit blocks (The Hoard) pulse with a `SEGMENTATION_FAULT` warning (Red Strobe). His own hands tremble as the GPU load of his internal HUD spikes to 99%.

2. MESH-BREAK (THE DISSOLUTION):
The "flowing away like water" is a `COLLISION_MESH_FAILURE`. The physics engine stops calculating "Solid" states for unverified assets. The gold ingots do not melt thermally; they undergo `VOXEL_DECOUPLING`. The rigid geometry scatters into millions of unconnected particles that behave like a fluid because they lack structural binding data. They clip through tables and hands (Z-Fighting artifacts visible) before being garbage-collected by the system.

3. PACING-STRATEGIE (THE DECAY):
00-15s (The Bloat): Slow, heavy pans. High inertia. The frame is crowded with assets. 24fps.
15-30s (The Ping): Sudden rhythmic cuts synced to data-packet transmission sounds. The "Prayer" uploads are burst-transmissions.
30-60s (The Purge): Variable frame rate. The "Liquefaction" is shown in extreme slow motion (120fps) to emphasize the physics simulation, while the "Sinners" reacting to it are sped up (time-lapse jitter), showing their powerlessness against the system clock.

4. AUDIO-DESIGN (THE FREQUENCY):
No choirs. The "Prayer of the Righteous" is the sound of a 56k Modem Handshake slowed down by 800% (a deep, digital groan).
The "Judgment" is the sound of a hard drive head crash (metallic screech) mixed with a fan spin-down.
The "Flowing" is the sound of thousands of glass shards falling on a tile floor, reversed.

5. TRANSITIONS (THE RENDER PASS):
`LOD_POPPING`. The transition between wealth and nothingness isn't a fade. It's a hard cut where the Level of Detail (LOD) drops instantly from "Ultra" to "Low" to "Null". The gold looks photorealistic one frame, then like a low-poly proxy the next, then vanishes.
