1. BIO-INJECTION (THE PARALLAX):
Henoch's optical upgrade is the `ASSET_VALUE_FILTER`. His vision does not tunnel; it desaturates. He perceives the "weight" of these objects not as mass, but as `MEMORY_LEAK_WARNINGS` overlaying his peripheral vision.

2. MESH-BREAK (THE DISSOLUTION):
The "flowing away like water" (Segment 10) is interpreted as `PHYSICS_ENGINE_FAILURE`. The gold and silver do not melt via heat. They lose their "Solid State" property flag. The molecular bonds of the wealth-assets are set to `NULL`. Hard currency instantly transitions to a zero-viscosity fluid, then sublimates into raw code-vapor that drifts upward (The Ascension of Wealth), ignoring local gravity.
