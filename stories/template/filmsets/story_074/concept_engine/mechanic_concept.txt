[ANALYSIS_OF_V1_FLAWS]
1.  **Magic vs. Tech:** The previous "Hologram" interaction was too generic fantasy/sci-fi. V2 replaces "floating holograms" with direct "Augmented Reality Viewport Overrides" where the world itself becomes the graph.
2.  **Physics:** The Merkabah was too passive. V2 defines it as a Gyroscopic Stabilizer Platform keeping them geostationary while the earth spins.
3.  **The Lag:** Previously just visual distance. V2 visualizes the Moon's lag as "Frame Drop" and "Texture Pop-in" to emphasize it is a rendering artifact of a slower subsystem.

[BIO_INJECTION_MECHANICS]
Technology: SYNAPTIC_OVERWRITE_CABLE.
Manifestation: Uriel does not just touch. A physical filament (fiber-optic needle) extends from Uriel’s fingertip and interfaces with a port at the base of Henoch's neck. Henoch's eyes roll back, displaying white noise static before the gold HUD boots up directly on the cornea.

[MESH_BREAK_VISUALS]
Technology: BACKEND_VISIBILITY_MODE.
Manifestation: The Skybox doesn't just turn transparent. It flickers. We see the "Backstage" of the universe: massive cooling pipes and server racks running behind the blue curtain of the sky. The "Gates" are heat-exhaust ports.

[PACING_STRATEGY]
Style: ASYNCHRONOUS_PULSE.
Rhythm: Two competing beats. A heavy, slow beat (Sun/Mainframe) vs. a slightly faster, erratic beat (Moon/Client). The edit aligns with the Sun beat, making the Moon cuts feel "off" and jarring, creating subconscious tension.

[AUDIO_DESIGN]
Primary Layer: 60Hz HUM (Mains electricity).
Secondary Layer: GEIGER_COUNTER_CLICKS (representing the counting of days).
Specific SFX: The sound of a heavy vault door sealing (The Gates).
Voice: Uriel’s voice is panning rapidly between Left and Right channels, simulating a stereo-test.

[TRANSITIONS]
Style: DATAMOSH_GLITCH.
Effect: When switching perspectives or timeframes, the pixels drag and smear (compression artifacting) before snapping into the new image.

[SPATIAL_CONTEXT_&_ARRIVAL]
Method: MERKABAH_GYRO.
Visual: A tetrahedonal craft made of spinning glass triangles. It locks into a "Grid Coordinate" in the upper atmosphere. The clouds below rush by at 1000mph, but inside the field, the air is dead still.
