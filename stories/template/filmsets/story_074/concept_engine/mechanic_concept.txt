[BIO_INJECTION_MECHANICS]
Technology: OCULAR_OVERWRITE_PROTOCOL.
Manifestation: Henoch's sclera turns entirely black. A gold rotating HUD (Head-Up Display) projects directly from the pupil outwards into the air (volumetric light projection). The data stream causes involuntary REM (Rapid Eye Movement) spasms as the information is written to his optical nerve. Veins in the temple pulse with bioluminescent blue coolant fluid to counteract the heat of processing.

[MESH_BREAK_VISUALS]
Technology: SKYBOX_RENDER_LAYER_TOGGLE.
Manifestation: The "Blue Sky" is revealed to be a high-resolution OLED dome. When Uriel explains the "Gates," the blue texture renders as a wireframe mesh in specific hexagonal sectors. The "Gates" are not physical holes but black, void-like rendering errors where the physics engine allows celestial objects to pass through the collision mesh.

[PACING_STRATEGY]
Style: METRONOMIC_PRECISION.
Rhythm: The edit cuts precisely on the "beat" of a cosmic clock (60 BPM). Long, stable tracking shots of the celestial bodies (Sun/Moon) moving on rails, interrupted by 3-frame "glitch" cuts of pure data scrolling. It feels like watching a precise machine operating, not a nature documentary.

[AUDIO_DESIGN]
Primary Layer: LOW_FREQUENCY_OSCILLATOR (LFO) - A constant 40Hz hum representing the Earth's rotation.
Secondary Layer: HIGH_VOLTAGE_ARCING - When the Sun passes a Gate.
Specific SFX: No choirs. Instead, the sound of massive cooling fans spinning up and the "clunk-hiss" of hydraulic locks engaging when days are counted.
Voice: Uriel's voice is synthesized, multi-layered, lacking breath.

[TRANSITIONS]
Style: LIDAR_SWEEP.
Effect: A vertical laser-scan line moves across the screen. Behind the line, the texture mode changes (e.g., from Photoreal to Thermal to Wireframe).

[SPATIAL_CONTEXT_&_ARRIVAL]
Method: MERKABAH_ELEVATOR.
Visual: Henoch and Uriel stand on a triangular platform of transparent hard-light (glass-like). They are at the Kármán line (100km altitude). The curvature of the Earth is visible below. They are stationary relative to the rotating Earth.
