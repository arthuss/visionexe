CONCEPT_CHAPTER_52_MECHANICS_V2

1. BIO-INJECTION
Manifestation: Ocular Overlay & Haptic Feedback.
Enoch's visual cortex receives a high-bandwidth feed directly from the Merkabah's sensor array. His eyes project a grid-overlay onto the physical world, tagging materials with chemical symbols (Fe, Cu, Ag, Au, Sn, Pb). The "whirlwind" transport induces vestibulocochlear dissonance—he stumbles upon landing not from fatigue, but from lag between his proprioception and the server's tick rate.

2. MESH-BREAK
Mechanism: Voxel Liquefaction & Texture Streaming Failure.
The "melting" is a forced state-change of the environment assets. The hard-surface geometry of the server-mountains loses its collision mesh data. The polygons detach and flow like a high-viscosity fluid simulation. The "water" is liquid nitrogen/dielectric coolant venting from ruptured containment units within the Gold Mountain structure.

3. PACING-STRATEGIE
Rhythm: Kinetic Entry -> Analytical Scan -> Dialog Interlock -> System Purge.
00s-05s: High-G deceleration and impact. Kinetic and rough.
05s-25s: Stabilized, methodical pans of the 6 structures. Clinical and observant.
25s-40s: Shot-Reverse-Shot dialog. Static camera, focus on UI/Face.
40s-60s: Catastrophic structural failure. Slow-motion fluid dynamics, heavy physics.

4. AUDIO-DESIGN
Soundscape: Data Center Ambience.
The wind is the roar of massive cooling fans. The mountains emit a 60Hz mains hum, varying in pitch based on mass. The "melting" sounds like metal fatigue groaning, high-pressure steam venting, and the wet slap of heavy slag. The Angel's voice is synthesized, lacking breath sounds, spatially locked to the center channel.

5. ÜBERGÄNGE
Transition: Merkabah Drop-In.
Simulates a "no-clip" mode disengaging. Enoch drops from a high altitude Z-axis coordinate to the ground plane. The transition frames show the texture resolution popping from LOD-0 (blurry satellite map) to LOD-5 (hyper-real obsidian grain) instantly upon contact.

6. SPATIAL CONTEXT (ARRIVAL)
Method: Orbital Insertion.
Enoch is payload. The Merkabah is the delivery vehicle.
Establishing Shot: Top-down view of the "Sector West" – a black mirror plane under a twilight sky, dominated by the 6 geometric monoliths arranged in a hexagonal lattice.

7. DIALOG-CHECK
Status: Active / Mandatory.
Query-Response protocol required to unlock the context data.
Sequence:
1. Enoch initiates query (Input).
2. Angel accesses database (Processing).
3. Angel displays overlay explanation (Output).
