CONCEPT: NULL_SPACE_EXCEPTION & THE_QUARANTINE_SECTOR (VERSION 2.0)

1. BIO-INJECTION (THE USER ERROR)
Manifestation: Haptic Desynchronization & Texture Failure.
Enoch's physical avatar cannot resolve the local lighting data because there is none. His skin does not "glow"; it fails to render shading, appearing flat and full-bright (unlit shader).
Ocular Impact: The HUD crashes. We see his iris aperture rapidly cycling through ISO settings, trying to find exposure in a zone with #000000 ambient light.
Physical Reaction: Not tremors, but "Packet Loss". His movement is jerky, skipping frames (interpolating from position A to C without B).

2. MESH-BREAK (THE GEOMETRY FAILURE)
The "Chaos" is a Null-Pointer Exception.
Visual: It is not "empty space"; it is "undefined space". When Enoch looks down, he sees the "Skybox Seam" — the jagged line where the graphical world ends and the void begins.
Geometry: Polygons here have no normals. They are single-sided. If Enoch turns his head, objects disappear because he is looking at their transparent back-faces.
Ground: There is no collider. He walks on a debug-grid that only renders within a 5-meter radius around his feet.

3. PACING-STRATEGIE
Rhythm: Latency Drag -> Buffer Snap.
Observation Shots (The Void/Stars): 24fps slowed to 12fps. Simulates high CPU load/lag. The camera movements are heavy, drunk, delayed.
Dialogue/Interaction (Uriel): Instant 0-frame cuts. No transitions. Pure "Jump Cuts" to simulate the System Admin (Uriel) forcing the narrative forward despite the lag.
Total Clips: 16 Clips.

4. AUDIO-DESIGN
The "Scream of the Silicon".
Base Layer: A raw 60Hz electrical mains hum (Ground Loop), distorted and amplified.
Event Layer: The sound of a corrupted hard drive "clicking" (The Click of Death) echoed into a vast canyon.
The Stars: They do not "burn" with fire sound; they emit the high-pitched frequency of a capacitor about to burst (Coil Whine).

5. ÜBERGÄNGE (TRANSITIONS)
Method: Noclip / Out-of-Bounds Glitch.
Enoch does not "fall" or "fly". He "clips".
Visual: He steps forward in the previous scene (Chapter 20), and his mesh passes through the wall texture. For a split second, we see the "Hall of Mirrors" effect (frame buffer not clearing) before the environment snaps to the absolute black of the Null Void.

6. SPATIAL CONTEXT (ARRIVAL)
Arrival: Spawn Point Relocation.
Enoch appears instantly in the void (T-Pose for 1 frame, then snaps to animation).
Establishing Shot: Not a wide pan (infinite nothingness is boring). Instead, a "glitch-in" reveal. The camera is black, then the 7 Stars render in a single frame, overloading the exposure.

7. DIALOG-CHECK
Dialogue: Essential for Verses 4-6 (The Query) and 8-10 (The Definition).
Technique: Asynchronous Audio.
We hear Uriel's voice clear and dry (Direct Line), but Enoch's lips move with a slight delay (Audio/Video Desync), emphasizing the instability of this sector.
Shot Pattern: Over-the-shoulder (Enoch) -> Low Angle (Uriel) -> ECU (Enoch's Glitching Eye).
