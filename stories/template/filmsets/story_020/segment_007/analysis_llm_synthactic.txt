*   `ቅዱሳን`: "holy".
*   `ዘይትቤቀሎ`: `ዘ-` (relative) + `ይትቤቀል` (verb, 3sg imperfect "he takes vengeance") + `-o` (object suffix, 3sg masc "him/it"). "who takes vengeance on it".
*   `ለኣለም`: `ለ-` (preposition "on/for") + `ኣለም` ("world").
*   `ወለብርሃናት`: `ወ-` (and) + `ለ-` (on/for) + `ብርሃናት` ("lights").
*   Structure: "Raguel (is) one of the holy angels, who takes vengeance on the world and on the lights."
*   The relative clause `ዘይትቤቀሎ፡​ለኣለም፡​ወለብርሃናት` modifies `ራጉኤል`.
*   Inside the relative clause: `ይትቤቀሎ` is the verb. The subject is `ዘ-` (who). The object is `-o`. The indirect objects are `ለኣለም` and `ለብርሃናት`.
