CONCEPT_MECHANICS_V2.0

ANALYSIS_V1.0_CRITIQUE
The previous iteration relied too heavily on "fantasy" metaphors (jewels, magical glowing).
*   **Deficit 1:** The "Fragrance" was treated as smell, not data ingestion via aerosolized nanites.
*   **Deficit 2:** The "Fire Mountain" was visual magma, not a functional thermal exhaust system for a high-performance cluster.
*   **Deficit 3:** Michael's appearance was too "angelic knight," lacking the "SysAdmin" aesthetic of the exeget:os layer.
*   **Correction:** Shift to Hard Sci-Fi / Server Farm semantics. The environment is a Cooling Loop and Processing Array.

CONCEPT_MECHANICS_V2.0_DEFINITIONS

1. BIO-INJECTION (The Smell)
**Mechanism:** Aerosolized Firmware Update.
**Tech:** The "Fragrance" is a dense cloud of golden smart-dust (nanites). When Enoch inhales, these are not processed by the olfactory bulb but bridge directly into the neural cortex via the nasal cavity.
**Visual:** The particles form a visible stream. Upon inhalation, Enoch's marble skin becomes transparent at the throat and lungs, revealing internal fiber-optics lighting up gold as the payload is accepted.

2. MESH-BREAK (The Fire Mountain)
**Mechanism:** Thermal Throttling & Heat Sink.
**Tech:** The "burning" mountain is the central heat exchanger for the cluster. The air around it is distorted not just by heat (mirage) but by local gravity distortions caused by immense energy consumption.
**Visual:** No smoke. Only high-velocity venting of ionized plasma and superheated coolant gas. The "shimmer" causes background rendering errors (pixel displacement) in the camera feed.

3. PACING-STRATEGIE (The Render Pipeline)
**Sequence:**
*   **Phase 1 (Spawn):** 0-5s. Chaotic. Voxel-to-Mesh construction. Glitch artifacts.
*   **Phase 2 (Inspection):** 5-45s. Smooth, mechanical camera movements (Drone/Slider). High FPS. showcasing the stability of the immutable infrastructure.
*   **Phase 3 (Interaction):** 45-80s. Locked-off shots. Shot-Reverse-Shot. Focus on the data exchange between User and Admin.

4. AUDIO-DESIGN (The Server Floor)
**Soundscape:**
*   **Ambient:** The defining sound is *Airflow*. Massive, laminar wind tunnel noise (Cooling Systems).
*   **Events:** The "Fire Mountain" emits a low-frequency 50Hz mains hum (Sub-Bass). The Crystal Mountains emit high-frequency coil whine (15kHz) that modulates with data processing load.
*   **Dialogue:** Michael's voice is not acoustic. It is a direct-to-auditory-cortex transmission, accompanied by digital packet-loss static.

5. TRANSITIONS (Socket Switching)
**Method:** No flying. Coordinate modification.
**Visual:** The previous scene "folds" away into 2D fractal geometry. The new scene (Node 24) builds up from a wireframe grid on the floor. Enoch remains stationary in world-space while the environment swaps around him.

6. FRAMEWORK & TIMING
**Duration:** 80-90 Seconds.
**Clip Count:** 18 Clips.
**Rationale:** The Shot-Reverse-Shot dialogue (Verses 5-6) requires distinct angles to convey the hierarchy between User (Enoch) and Admin (Michael). The complexity of the "Seven Mountains" layout requires multiple establishing pans.

7. DIALOG-CHECK
**Status:** Active.
**Speakers:** Enoch (User Query) -> Michael (Admin Response).
**Format:** Telepathic/Digital. Visors/Eyes pulse in sync with transmission.

8. SPATIAL CONTEXT (ARRIVAL)
**Method:** Instant Instancing.
**Visual:** Enoch appears in a T-Pose for 1 frame, then snaps into animation.
**Layout:** He stands on an obsidian platform. To his left (East): 3 Red/Blue Nodes. To his right (North): 3 Metallic/Dark Nodes. Center: The White Throne Node.
