CONCEPT: SECTOR_NULL_POINTER [THE_ACCURSED_VALLEY]

1. BIO-INJECTION
Enoch's ocular implants shift to `DEBUG_MODE_RED`. His irises expand, projecting a cone of red laser-grid scanning light onto the terrain. The skin around his temples pulses with heat as his internal processor attempts to parse the corrupted geometry of the valley. He is not just seeing; he is reading the error logs of the land.

2. MESH-BREAK
The "Accursed Valley" is a region of `DATA_CORRUPTION`. The physics engine here is broken. Trees do not sway; they jitter in static stasis. The ground texture flickers between photorealistic mud and raw, unrendered grey polygons. Shadows are pixelated and detach from their objects. It is a "Dead Zone" in the simulation, flagged for formatting.

3. PACING-STRATEGIE
Oppressive and heavy. The camera moves with the weight of a surveillance drone in heavy gravity. Static shots of the environment that linger slightly too long, emphasizing the discomfort of the glitching reality. When Uriel speaks, the cuts are precise and clinical (Shot-Reverse-Shot), contrasting with the chaotic environment.

4. AUDIO-DESIGN
No choir. No wind. The soundscape is `BIT_CRUSHED_AMBIENCE`. A constant, low-fidelity static hiss (white noise) overlaid with the sound of a failing hard drive (rhythmic mechanical clicking). Uriel's voice cuts through with zero reverb, perfectly dry and synthetic.

5. ÜBERGÄNGE
`NOCLIP_DESCENT`. Enoch and Uriel do not walk down; they clip through the geometry of the surrounding mountains to arrive at the vantage point. The transition is a sudden shift in coordinates, causing a momentary "smear" of the previous frame before the new location snaps into focus.
