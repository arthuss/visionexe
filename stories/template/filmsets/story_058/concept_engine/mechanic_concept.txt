BIO-INJECTION
**System:** `RETINAL_OVERWRITE_PROTOCOL`
**Manifestation:** The bandwidth of the "Third Parable" exceeds the processing capacity of human biological optics.
**Physical Change:** Enoch's sclera burns away, replaced by cooling fluid (liquid gold). The iris is mechanically excised by nanobots in real-time, replaced by a multi-lens aperture system (`SOLAR_APERTURE_V2`). Subdermal circuitry patterns on his face ignite from dormant grey to active white-hot, visible through the skin as heat-sinks.

MESH-BREAK
**System:** `VOXEL_DECOMPOSITION_EVENT`
**Visual:** The "Darkness" (Old World data) does not fade; it suffers a polygon count failure. Shadows crystallize into jagged, low-poly obsidian shards.
**Dynamics:** As the new "Light" assets load, the old geometry creates collision errors. The black shards vibrate violently (physics glitch) before exploding into zero-point energy dust, cleared instantly by the system's refresh rate.

PACING-STRATEGIE
**Type:** `PROCESSING_LATENCY_SIMULATION`
**Rhythm:** Time dilation. The influx of data is so fast that physical time appears frozen.
**Edit Pattern:** Extremely slow, floating camera movements (Zero-G sensation) contrasted with rapid-fire data overlays (1-2 frame insert cuts of code).
**Speed:** 20% playback speed for physical movement, 500% speed for HUD elements/light pulses.

AUDIO-DESIGN
**Ambiance:** `QUANTUM_COMPUTER_IDLE`. A layering of thousands of cooling fans spinning in perfect unison, creating a "white noise" choir.
**High Frequency:** `DATA_SCREAM`. The sound of a dial-up modem slowed down by 800%, creating a melodic, metallic screeching representing the "Light".
**Events:** The destruction of darkness sounds like breaking glass in a vacuum—sharp, immediate, with no reverb tail.

ÜBERGÄNGE
**Method:** `LUMA_KEY_WIPE`.
**Execution:** The scene transitions not by fading, but by the brightest pixels of the current frame expanding until they consume the image, forming the white background of the next frame.
