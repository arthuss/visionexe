BIO-INJECTION
**System:** `OCULAR_COOLANT_FLUSH`
**Mechanism:** The upgrade to "Version 3.0" requires bandwidth exceeding biological limits. The "liquid gold" is a fluorocarbon-based immersion cooling fluid injected directly into the orbital socket to dissipate heat from the new `SOLAR_APERTURE_V2` optical chipset.
**Physical Change:** The organic eye is not cut; it is dissolved by a chemical agent (`SOLVENT_X`) and immediately replaced by the solid-state sensor array growing from the optic nerve outward. The skin around the eyes glows orange not from magic, but from thermal radiation (heat sinking).

MESH-BREAK
**System:** `GARBAGE_COLLECTION_CYCLE`
**Visual:** The "Darkness" is treated as unreferenced memory. It does not shatter like glass; it de-resolves.
**Dynamics:** Shadows and legacy geometry turn into wireframes (green lines), then point clouds, then NULL value (transparency). The "Light" is the new texture data overwriting these addresses in real-time.

PACING-STRATEGIE
**Type:** `BUFFER_UNDERRUN_SIMULATION`
**Rhythm:** The sequence begins with visual "lag"—stuttering freeze-frames (simulating a CPU bottleneck during the upgrade).
**Shift:** Once the upgrade completes (Clip 9), the framerate snaps to a hyper-fluid 144Hz look (simulated by smooth motion with zero motion blur), representing the "Timeless" state (removal of TTL).

AUDIO-DESIGN
**Ambiance:** `HVAC_SERVER_DRONE`. The sound of a massive data center—thousands of cooling fans creating a resonant chord.
**High Frequency:** `COIL_WHINE`. As the "Light" intensifies, a sharp electronic whine rises in pitch until it exits the audible spectrum, leaving a sensation of pressure.
**Events:** The deletion of darkness makes a `VACUUM_IMPLOSION` sound—a sudden, sharp removal of all bass frequencies, creating a "digital silence."

ÜBERGÄNGE
**Method:** `DATAMOSH_WIPE`
**Execution:** Pixel sorting algorithms. The texture information of the previous scene is "dragged" by motion vectors into the geometry of the new scene before snapping into the correct UV map.
