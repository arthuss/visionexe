TECHNICAL CONCEPT & VISUAL SCRIPT: CHAPTER 57 (VERSION 2.0)

[TECHNICAL_MECHANICS]

1. BIO-INJECTION (OPTICAL OVERRIDE):
The "Vision" is a Retinal Projection Overlay. High-intensity lasers write data directly onto Enoch's optic nerve.
Physical Manifestation: The iris widens to encompass the entire eye. The pupil displays a rapid-fire data stream (QR-code-like flickering). Excessive heat from the projection causes "Coolant Discharge" – a clear, viscous, synthetic fluid leaking from the tear ducts to prevent retinal burn-in.

2. MESH-BREAK (LOAD INSTABILITY):
The "Shaking of the Pillars" is a Physics Engine Stress Test. The arrival of thousands of high-poly assets (the Chariots) simultaneously causes a CPU bottleneck.
Visuals: The "Pillars" (Mountains/Foundations) vibrate at a sub-pixel level (aliasing jitter). Rocks lose their texture maps temporarily, revealing the wireframe topology underneath. The ground does not just shake; it "clips" – solid objects pass through each other due to collision detection lag.

3. PACING-STRATEGIE (TRAFFIC SPIKE):
Phase 1 (Signal Detection): Extremely slow zooms. Silence. Waiting for the handshake.
Phase 2 (Bandwidth Saturation): Fast, frantic cuts matching the speed of the incoming data packets (the Chariots).
Phase 3 (Buffer Clear): Sudden, absolute stillness as the fleet docks and the simulation stabilizes.

4. AUDIO-DESIGN (THE HANDSHAKE):
The "Noise of Chariots" is a raw Data-Handshake Signal amplified to 140dB. It is not an engine roar, but a synthesized, rhythmic "Modem Screech" mixed with the deep, vibrating hum of a massive server farm cooling system. It is the sound of processing power.

5. TRANSITIONS (AUGMENTED REALITY):
From Reality to Vision: "HUD Initialization". The sky is overlaid with a vector grid. Target markers lock onto the incoming objects.
From Vision to Reality: "UI Fade". The grid dissolves, leaving the physical objects (Chariots) rendered in the scene.

6. SPATIAL CONTEXT (THE NODE):
Enoch is at a "Terminal Node" (The South). He is not moving; the network traffic is routing *to* him. The "East and West" are data pipelines visible in the sky.
