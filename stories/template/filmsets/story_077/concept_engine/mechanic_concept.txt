*   **Geometry Error:** The "Seven Mountains" were visualized as terrain. They must be **Infrastructure Components** (Heat Spreaders/Cryo-Towers) that pierce the simulation layer. Frost is not weather; it is **Liquid Nitrogen Condensation**.
*   **Routing Bug:** The river paths were vague. Verse 5-7 is a specific circuit diagram. The coolant loops have distinct start/end points (West->Great Sea, North->Erythraean) which dictate thermal exchange directionality.
