1. MECHANICS & PHYSICS

BIO-INJECTION:
Neural-Lace Overheat. Enoch's interface ports at the temples smoke slightly. Subdermal circuitry glows white-hot through the skin, tracing the nervous system. His eyes do not just scan; they project a physical cone of red LIDAR light that maps the geometry of the dissolving world.

MESH-BREAK:
LOD-Cascade Failure. The destruction is not physical crumbling, but a rapid degradation of Level-of-Detail. High-fidelity rock textures pop into flat shaded polygons, then into green wireframes, then into void. The physics engine detaches; objects float or clip through each other (Z-fighting).

PACING-STRATEGIE:
Glitch-Rhythm. The edit follows a pattern of "Buffer Underrun". Smooth 60fps motion is interrupted by freeze-frames (0.2s) and macro-blocking artifacts. The sequence accelerates: long establishing shots (4s) -> rapid destruction cuts (1s) -> final black screen.

AUDIO-DESIGN:
The "Voice of God" is replaced by a Global System Alert: a rhythmic, deafening industrial klaxon mixed with the sound of a hard drive read-head scratching a platter. The "trembling earth" sounds like deep, distorted bass clipping (square wave).

ÜBERGÄNGE:
V-Sync Tearing. Transitions between the stable world and the glitch world happen via horizontal screen tearing. The new scene "slides" in from the top, misaligned, before snapping into place.

FRAMEWORK & TIMING:
Total Duration: 65s.
Clip Count: 16 Clips.
Structure: 3-Act structure (Warning -> Glitch -> Deletion).
Buffer: 5 seconds for "Connection Terminated" static.

DIALOG-CHECK:
No spoken dialogue. System Text-to-Speech only (Voice-Over). Enoch is a silent observer, recording the crash logs.

SPATIAL CONTEXT (ARRIVAL):
Instant Node-Switch. No travel. One frame Enoch is in the previous location, the next frame he is standing on the "Debug Platform" – a transparent, collision-only plane hovering above the map coordinates of the destruction.
