1. MECHANICS & PHYSICS

BIO-INJECTION:
Direct Neural Shunt. Henoch does not just see; he processes. A fiber-optic cable extends from the base of his skull into the `OBSIDIAN_SLATE`. His eyes roll back, replaced by internal HUD projections visible through the translucent marble eyelids. The skin around the port sites turns bruised purple (heat trauma).

MESH-BREAK:
Polygon Jitter & Texture Streaming Failure. The world doesn't just crack; the vertex positions lose floating-point precision. Objects vibrate intensely before snapping to grid coordinates. High-res textures fail to load (MIP-mapping error), reverting to checkerboard "Missing Texture" patterns before turning to wireframe.

PACING-STRATEGIE:
Quantized Stutter. The frame rate of the environment drops to 12fps while Henoch remains at 60fps. This creates a jarring visual disconnect. Cuts are synchronized to a "glitch" beat—black frames inserted between scenes to simulate data packet loss.

AUDIO-DESIGN:
The "Voice of God" is replaced by a massive "Cooling Fan Spool-Up" sound, layering with the high-pitched whine of coil capacitors under load. The "Earthquake" is the sound of digital clipping (Square Wave Distortion) and hard drive seek-arm thrashing.

ÜBERGÄNGE:
Hex-Dump Bleed. Transitions utilize a vertical "waterfall" of hexadecimal code that washes over the screen, dissolving one scene into the next. The reality doesn't fade; it is overwritten.

FRAMEWORK & TIMING:
Total Duration: 70s.
Clip Count: 16 Clips.
Structure: Initialization -> Error Propagation -> Deletion -> Quarantine.
Buffer: 3 seconds pre-roll for connection handshake.

DIALOG-CHECK:
No verbal dialogue. Text-only communication via the Slate UI (Log output). Henoch observes the runtime execution.

SPATIAL CONTEXT (ARRIVAL):
Socket Connection. Henoch is inserted into the scene via a "T-Pose" snap. One frame empty space, next frame he is rigid, then animates into a relaxed stance. No travel animation; pure asset loading.
