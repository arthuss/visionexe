2. MESH-BREAK (INFINITE LOD)

As the camera pushes in, the bark resolves from texture to geometry, to molecular lattice, to streaming raw binary code. It is a singularity of infinite resolution—a "glitch" of perfection.

Edit Pattern: Analytical Shot-Reverse-Shot for the handshake/dialogue. Slow, linear dolly movements for the inspection of hardware (Tree/Mountain).
Flow: Query -> Analysis -> Restriction -> Projection -> Sync.

Ambience: A constant 40Hz sub-bass drone (The Mountain acting as a geothermal generator/heatsink).
The Tree: Emits a 12kHz crystalline data-screech, modulated into a pleasant harmonic resonance by Enoch’s audio filters.
Voice: Michael’s voice is a multi-layered synthetic polyphony, heavily processed with metallic reverb.

5. TRANSITIONS (FOCUS PULL)
No cuts between realities. The transition is optical.
Technique: Rack Focus. The background (World) blurs into bokeh data-points; the foreground (Tree) sharpens into hyper-reality. AR Overlays snap into existence (`LOCK_STATUS: ACTIVE`).
