THEME: Garbage Collection, Priority Escalation, Kernel Integrity Verification.

1. BIO-INJECTION MECHANICS
Manifestation: Haptic Command Syntax.
Visual: Henoch does not cast spells; he inputs code. His hands are equipped with "Null-Touch" gloves—semi-transparent polygon meshes that override local physics. When he issues a command, he isn't waving; he is manipulating invisible UI sliders in the air. The "injection" is a wireless packet burst from his optic nerve transmitter to the local subnet. We see the air shimmer with heat distortion around his head as the CPU in his cortex overclocks.

2. MESH-BREAK PHYSICS
Phenomenon: Level-of-Detail (LOD) Stripping.
Visual: The destruction of Sinners is an optimization process. The Engine determines they are no longer "Hero Assets" but "Background Trash."
Step 1: Texture Unbinding. Their 8k skin textures detach, floating inches off their bodies like peeled wallpaper.
Step 2: Polygon Reduction. The mesh collapses from 500k polys to 500 polys in a single frame.
Step 3: Culling. The rendering engine simply stops drawing them. They flash white, then vanish, leaving a "Missing Asset" void (bright magenta silhouette) for one second before total deletion.

3. PACING STRATEGY
Rhythm: Latency Gap.
Technique:
The Righteous (Optimized): 120fps. Smooth, fluid, responsive.
The Sinners (Throttled): 5fps. Stroboscopic motion. They freeze for 10 frames, then snap to a new position. This visualizes their lack of processing priority.
Editing: The cuts align with the "Lag Spikes." We cut on the freeze, creating a jarring, rhythmic disorientation.

4. AUDIO DESIGN
Soundscape: Signal-to-Noise Ratio.
Replacement:
"Healing Voice" -> Pure Sine Wave (432Hz) & White Noise ventilation hum (Clean Room ambience).
"Woe/Sirens" -> Square Wave Clipping, Modem Handshake screeches, and the sound of heavy ceramic plates grinding (Hard Drive failure).

5. TRANSITIONS
Method: Render Mode Cycling.
Visual: The camera does not cut; the *renderer* switches modes. The scene flickers from "Lit" (Final Render) to "Buffer Visualization" (G-Buffer) showing Normal Maps, Depth Maps, and Heat Maps of the subjects. This reveals the "Sinners" as cold/dead in the Heat Map while the Righteous glow hot.

7. DIALOG CHECK
Status: System Text Only.
Execution: No spoken words. Subtitles appear as "System Notifications" in the center of the screen, styled like kernel panic logs or boot sequences. Font: Monospace Terminal.

8. SPATIAL CONTEXT (ARRIVAL)
Method: Socket Connection.
Visual: No walking. Henoch connects to the scene. The environment loads *around* him. First the floor grid, then the walls, then the lighting. He is the static constant; the world is the variable.
