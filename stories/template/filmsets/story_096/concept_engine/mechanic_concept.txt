CONCEPT_TITLE: SYSTEM_PURGE_AND_OPTIMIZATION_PROTOCOL_V2
CHAPTER: 96
THEME: Garbage Collection, Priority Escalation, Kernel Integrity Verification.

1. BIO-INJECTION MECHANICS
Manifestation: Sub-Dermal Firmware Update.
Visual: The "Healing" is not magical regeneration but a visible re-writing of damaged code. On the skin of the Righteous, open wounds or corrupted texture patches are swarmed by silver nanites. These nanites weave new polygon meshes in real-time, sealing breaches with a gold-filament suture that fades into healthy skin. It is a "Hotfix" applied to a running system.

2. MESH-BREAK PHYSICS
Phenomenon: Level-of-Detail (LOD) Collapse.
Visual: The "destruction" of the Sinners is a forced optimization. The system identifies them as "Bloatware." Their rendering priority is set to zero.
Phase 1: Texture stripping (4K textures vanish, leaving flat grey surfaces).
Phase 2: Polygon reduction (Smooth curves become jagged low-poly shapes).
Phase 3: Vertex dissolution (The wireframe disconnects and scatters like dust).

3. PACING STRATEGY
Rhythm: Latency Contrast.
Technique:
The Righteous Timeline: 60fps, fluid motion, perfect frame-pacing. Camera moves are motivated and smooth (Steadicam/Dolly).
The Sinner Timeline: Lag simulation. Their movements stutter, loop, and rubber-band. The camera shakes violently during their deletion events, simulating a GPU crash.

4. AUDIO DESIGN
Soundscape: The Data Center.
Replacement:
"Voice of Rest" -> The pure, unwavering 432Hz sine wave of a perfectly balanced quantum core. A sound that cancels out background noise.
"Woe/Destruction" -> The screech of cooling fans spinning off-axis, the click-of-death from failing mechanical drives, and the distorted static of a corrupted audio buffer.

5. TRANSITIONS
Method: Debug View Overlay.
Visual: The transition from "Appearance" (Riches) to "Reality" (Sin) is visualized by a "Wireframe Pass." A vertical scanline sweeps across the screen. Behind the line, the render mode switches from "Lit" to "Shader Complexity" (Red = Bad/Heavy, Green = Good/Optimized).

6. FRAMEWORK & TIMING
Total Duration: 75 Seconds (Extended for multi-shot sequences).
Clip Count: 16 Clips.
Structure:
Clips 1-3: The Admin Observation (Enoch initiates).
Clips 4-7: The Ascent (Righteous Evacuation).
Clips 8-10: The Scan (Revealing the Corruption).
Clips 11-13: The Consumption (Bad Data).
Clips 14-16: The Purge (Deletion).

7. DIALOG CHECK
Status: System Broadcast Only.
Execution: No lip-sync. Text overlays in the HUD: "WARNING: INTEGRITY FAILURE DETECTED" and "PRIORITY USER: ACCESS GRANTED."

8. SPATIAL CONTEXT (ARRIVAL)
Method: Layer Shift.
Visual: Enoch is already in the city but shifts his rendering layer to "Admin/Spectator." He becomes semi-translucent to the crowd, walking through them without collision, observing the code beneath the skin.
