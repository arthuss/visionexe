CONCEPT_TITLE: SYSTEM_PURGE_AND_OPTIMIZATION_PROTOCOL_V2
CHAPTER: 96
THEME: Garbage Collection, Priority Escalation, Kernel Integrity Verification.

1. BIO-INJECTION MECHANICS
Manifestation: Voxel-Rewrite & Texture Re-Baking.
Visual: "Healing" is not biological regeneration. It is a "Restore from Backup." Damaged skin on the Righteous does not heal; it pixelates into small cubic voxels which are deleted, then immediately replaced by pristine, high-resolution geometry instanced from the master file. It looks like a digital block-build effect occurring at a microscopic level.

2. MESH-BREAK PHYSICS
Phenomenon: Geometry Decimation & Texture Streaming Failure.
Visual: The destruction of Sinners is a "Loss of Definition." They are de-prioritized by the engine.
Phase 1 (Warning): Mipmap collapse. Their high-res textures fail to load, leaving them blurry and pixelated.
Phase 2 (Collapse): Polygon reduction. Their silhouette snaps from smooth organic curves to jagged N64-era low-poly models.
Phase 3 (Null): Vertex scattering. The wireframe unbinds, and the vertices drift apart like dust in zero-g.

3. PACING STRATEGY
Rhythm: Frame-Rate Dissonance.
Technique:
The Admin (Henoch) & Righteous: Rendered at 144fps. Fluid, hyper-real motion.
The Corrupt (Sinners): Rendered at 12fps. Their motion is jerky, missing frames, exhibiting "rubber-banding" (snapping back to previous positions) to visualize their disconnection from the server clock.
Editing: Cuts occur on the glitch—hard digital jump cuts rather than smooth fades.

4. AUDIO DESIGN
Soundscape: The Server Floor.
Replacement:
"Voice of Rest" -> Active Noise Cancellation. The sudden, absolute silence of a server room when all fans spin down simultaneously. 0dB floor.
"Woe/Destruction" -> Coil Whine, HDD Head Crash (rhythmic metallic clicking), and Bit-Crushed distortion peaking in the red.

5. TRANSITIONS
Method: Render Pass Visualization.
Visual: The world does not fade. The view mode cycles. We see the "Wireframe" overlay flicker on top of the "Lit" render to reveal the underlying structure of the "Rich" sinners (showing them as hollow shells).

6. FRAMEWORK & TIMING
Total Duration: 90 Seconds.

Clips 17-18 (Seg 8): Garbage Collection (Deletion).

7. DIALOG CHECK
Status: No Voice Acting.
Execution: Hex-Code streams in the peripheral HUD. "ACCESS_VIOLATION" and "PERMISSION_GRANTED" flash in Ge'ez characters.

8. SPATIAL CONTEXT (ARRIVAL)
Method: Node-Hopping / No-Clip.
Visual: Henoch does not walk. He "instances" into the scene. One frame he is not there, the next frame he is, standing perfectly still. He is in "Spectator Mode," clipping slightly through a wall to show he is outside the physics engine.
