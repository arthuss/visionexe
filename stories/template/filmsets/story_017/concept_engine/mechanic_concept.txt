1. BIO-INJECTION MECHANICS
Subject: Henoch_v0.8 (Observer Class).
Manifestation: "Radiant Heat Throttling". Due to proximity to the System Perimeter (Firewall), the subject's internal cooling systems are visible.
Visual: The "Marble Skin" shader becomes 60% opaque. Beneath the surface, a vascular system of glowing blue coolant fluid pulses rapidly (liquid nitrogen circulation).
Safety Protocol: When approaching high-voltage zones (Verse 1), a refractive "Heat Haze" aura surrounds Henoch, distorting the air around him—a localized deflector shield.
Ocular Upgrade: Eyes shift to "Debug Mode" (Wireframe overlay on retinas) to interpret the raw energy forms.

2. MESH-BREAK DYNAMICS
Phenomenon: "Raw Data Flux". The environment is not solid matter; it is uncompiled code.
Geometry Behavior: "Vertex Displacement". The ground near the "Rivers of Fire" does not melt; it glitches. Polygons flicker between stone texture and wireframe grid.
The "Mountain whose head reaches heaven" (Verse 2) is a Space Elevator / Uplink Cable. Where it touches the "Sky" (The Skybox), the clouds act like a flat ceiling texture with visible pixelation, indicating the edge of the renderable map.

3. PACING STRATEGY
Rhythm: "Packet Loss Stutter".
Movement: Travel between sectors is instantaneous but violent. One frame Henoch is in a desert, the next in a blizzard. No fade-outs.
Observation: Static, tripod-locked camera shots for environments to emphasize scale. Handheld, shaky-cam for Henoch's reactions to emphasize disorientation.
Contrast: The stillness of the "Dead Zones" (Winter/Darkness) vs. the chaotic noise of the "Fire Zones".

4. AUDIO DESIGN
Ambience: "Industrial Drone".
Layer 1 (Base): The sound of a massive ventilation system (White Noise).
Layer 2 (The Fire): High-voltage electric buzzing (60Hz hum) and the crackle of arc welding.
Layer 3 (The Water): Hydraulic pumps cycling and fluid rushing through high-pressure pipes.
Voice: Uriel’s commands cut through with digital distortion, like a calm Air Traffic Controller on a radio frequency.

5. TRANSITIONS (NODE-HOPPING)
Method: "Render-Queue Jump".
Visual: The screen momentarily freezes (Datamosh), colors invert for 2 frames, and the new location loads instantly.
Effect: Simulates a user dragging a character across a map in a dev-editor.

6. FRAMEWORK & TIMING
Total Duration: 65 Seconds.
Clip Count: 16 Clips (Expanded for Reaction Shots and Split Actions).
Buffer: 2 "Signal Loss" transition clips included.

7. DIALOG STRATEGY
Interaction: Asymmetric.
Format: Uriel (The Admin) lectures. Henoch (The User) observes.
Shot-Reverse-Shot: Used in Clip 13/14 to ground the scale of the "Mouth of the Deep".
Shot A: Uriel pointing down.
Shot B: Henoch looking down, terrified.

8. SPATIAL CONTEXT (ARRIVAL)
Method: "Zero-Point Spawn".
Visual: No walking. Henoch appears in a T-Pose for 1 frame before snapping into an idle animation.
Establishing Shot: Each sector begins with a Macro-Wide shot of the infrastructure before cutting to Henoch.
