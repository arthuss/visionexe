1. BIO-INJECTION MECHANICS
Subject: Henoch_v0.8 (Observer Class).
Manifestation: Due to proximity to raw energy conduits ("Fire"), the subject's rendering is unstable.
Visual: "Glass-Skin" Shader. The epidermis is 80% transparent, revealing a glowing nervous system (fiber optics) beneath.
Safety Protocol: A hexagonal "Force-Field" overlay (orange wireframe) pulses over his skin when he approaches the thermal hazards (Verse 1).
Ocular Upgrade: Eyes switch to "Thermal/Spectral Vision" (solid black sclera, red iris rings) to perceive the raw data streams without blinding.

2. MESH-BREAK DYNAMICS
Phenomenon: The "Fire" described is not chemical combustion; it is raw plasma data.
Geometry Behavior: "Liquid Voxelization". Solid objects (mountains, ground) near the fire rivers do not crumble; they liquefy into flowing cubes before resolidifying.
The "Mountain whose head reaches heaven" (Verse 2) is a massive heat-sink tower. Its peak disappears into a pixelated cloud layer (The Skybox Ceiling), where the geometry simplifies into low-poly LODs (Level of Detail) due to render distance limits.

3. PACING STRATEGY
Rhythm: "Latency Spikes".
Movement: The travel between points ("And they took me...") is instantaneous. Hard cuts. No travel time.
Observation: Once arrived, the camera moves in slow, heavy, stabilized pans (Simulating a drone inspection).
Contrast: Rapid, glitch-like transitions between locations vs. slow, majestic terror of the environments.

4. AUDIO DESIGN
Ambience: No choirs. No wind.
Layer 1 (Base): A deep, resonant 50Hz hum (Server Room Drone) that vibrates the subwoofer.
Layer 2 (The Fire): The sound of high-voltage electrical arcing (Tesla Coil snaps) and the roar of a blast furnace.
Layer 3 (The Water): The "Living Waters" sound like liquid nitrogen boiling off—a high-pitched hiss.
Voice: Uriel's voice is heavily processed, sounding like it's coming through a pilot's headset (Bandpass filter).

5. TRANSITIONS (NODE-HOPPING)
Method: "Voxel-Dissolve".
Visual: The current environment freezes, turns into a wireframe grid, and dissolves upwards. The new environment slams down from above in solid blocks.
Effect: Disorienting but efficient. Emphasizes that Henoch is being moved by system administrators (The Angels).

6. FRAMEWORK & TIMING
Total Duration: 60 Seconds.
Clip Count: 15 Clips (High density due to multiple distinct locations).
Buffer: 3 "Glitch/Transition" filler clips included.

7. DIALOG STRATEGY
Interaction: Minimal. Instructional only.
Format: Uriel acts as the Tour Guide / System Admin.
Shot-Reverse-Shot:
Shot A: Henoch looking overwhelmed (Close-Up, reflection of fire in eyes).
Shot B: Uriel pointing at a structure (Wide Shot, back to camera).
Shot C: POV Shot of what Henoch sees (The Data River).

8. SPATIAL CONTEXT (ARRIVAL)
Method: "Hard Spawn".
Visual: Henoch does not walk into the scene. He pops into existence (Spawn). A small shockwave of dust displaces around his feet upon arrival.
Establishing Shot: Not needed for arrival. The first shot of each segment IS the environment (Wide), with Henoch spawning into it.
