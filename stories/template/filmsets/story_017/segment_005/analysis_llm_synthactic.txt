### Principles of Analysis

1.  **No Ambiguity Reduction**: All syntactically valid structural ambiguities are presented as distinct parses. This includes ambiguities in coordination scope, adjunct attachment, and the relationship between clauses (e.g., apposition vs. coordination).
2.  **Token Fidelity**: The analysis respects the provided tokenization. The internal structure of each sentence-token is analyzed, but the token itself is the unit of reference. Words within the `surface` string of a token are treated as the terminal nodes of the parse trees.
3.  **Formal Notation**: Each parse is represented using bracket notation to show constituency and a list of head-dependent relations to specify the grammatical functions.
