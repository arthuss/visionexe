Manifestation: OCULAR_TELEMETRY_OVERLAY.
Physical: Enoch's irises expand to cover the entire sclera, turning into rotating mechanical shutters (camera aperture style). A fine, glowing gold grid is laser-etched onto his cornea to facilitate precise angular measurements of the star trajectories.
Function: High-speed motion tracking and data logging. The "writing" is not manual but a direct neural dump from his optical nerve to the obsidian tablet via a wireless handshake.

2. MESH-BREAK
Phenomenon: POLYMORPHIC_INSTABILITY.
Visual: The "beasts" and "birds" described in the text are rendered as unstable assets with corrupted texture maps. Their geometry flickers between states (e.g., low-poly wireframe <-> high-res organic <-> voxel cloud) every few frames. This represents the "changing of form." The "ends of the earth" is the literal edge of the terrain mesh, where the texture abruptly stops, revealing the infinite black grid of the void beyond.

3. PACING-STRATEGIE
Rhythm: ANALYTICAL_STACCATO.
Structure: The sequence begins with chaotic, rapid cuts mirroring the unstable beasts (0.5s - 1s glitches). As Enoch turns to the Gates of Heaven (the structured data ports), the editing slows down to a precise, rhythmic metronome beat (4s clips), synchronized with the orderly exit of the stars.

4. AUDIO-DESIGN
Soundscape: PERIMETER_NOISE.
Ambience: A deep, distorted bass drone representing the unstable rendering engine at the edge of the world.
Specifics: The "changing voices" of the birds are represented by granular synthesis artifacts and glitch-stuttering audio. The "stars coming out" sound like high-speed pneumatic tubes or server rack cooling fans spinning up in sequence.

5. ÜBERGÄNGE
Method: RENDER_PASS_SWITCH.
Visual: Transitions between the chaotic beast-zone and the orderly gate-zone are handled by a "View Mode" switch effect (e.g., switching from "Lit" to "Collision Complexity" view). The world briefly strips down to green wireframe before re-rendering in the new context.
