CONCEPT DEFINITION 2.0

1. BIO-INJECTION MECHANICS
Manifestation: Direct Neural Interface (DNI) Overload.
Visual: Henoch is physically suspended in a "Haptic Rig" (Unit 77-B). A thick fiber-optic bundle is jacked into the base of his skull (Atlas vertebrae). His eyes are wide open but displaying "No Signal" static (Snow crash). The skin around the port is bruised purple, veins distended with black coolant fluid to prevent cerebral overheating during high-bandwidth data ingestion.

2. MESH-BREAK PHYSICS
Phenomenon: Firewall Penetration / Voxel Injection.
Visual: The "Sky" is a high-resolution LED dome (Skybox_V9). The "Stars" are not falling objects but burning pixels where the external virus (The Watchers) is burning through the display layer. Upon entry, they don't just fall; they "compile" in mid-air, gathering local voxels to build their physical avatars (The White Bulls) before impact. The ground doesn't ripple; it shatters into untextured polygons (purple/black checkerboard) at the impact site.

3. PACING STRATEGY
Rhythm: The Infection Curve.
Phase 1 (Latency): Static, observational shots. 24fps. Clinical detachment.
Phase 2 (Packet Loss): Glitch-cuts. Dropped frames. The movement of the entities stutters.
Phase 3 (System Crash): Chaos. Handheld virtual camera. Motion blur cranked up. 60fps converted to 24fps for a jagged, hyper-real look.

4. AUDIO DESIGN
Ambience: "The Hum" – A layered track of heavy server fans and high-voltage transformer buzz (50Hz).
Event Sounds:
- Star Fall: The sound of a jet engine stalling + digital artifact screeching.
- Transformation: Pneumatic pistons firing and ceramic plates locking into place.
- Screams: Corrupted audio files, time-stretched metallic grinding.

5. TRANSITIONS
Method: Buffer Underrun.
Visual: The image freezes, tears horizontally (Scanline error), and the next scene "loads" from top to bottom, overwriting the previous frame.

6. FRAMEWORK & TIMING
Total Duration: ~75 Seconds.
Clip Count: 16 Clips.
Structure:
- Clips 1-2: Henoch & The Interface.
- Clips 3-5: The First Payload (Star -> White Bull).
- Clips 6-7: The Herd (Black Units).
- Clips 8-9: The Mass Injection (Invasion).
- Clips 10-12: Hybridization & Birth (The Anomalies).
- Clips 13-15: The Purge (Cannibalism/Terror).
- Clip 16: Disconnect.

7. DIALOG CHECK
Status: NEGATIVE.
Reasoning: System Log. No audio drivers loaded for speech. Only telemetry data.

8. SPATIAL CONTEXT (ARRIVAL)
Method: Remote Desktop Protocol.
Visual: No travel. Henoch is the "Cursor". The world renders around him instantly. We start with a "Loading Geometry" wireframe pass that textures in <0.5 seconds.
