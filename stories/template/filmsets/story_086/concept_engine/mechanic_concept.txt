CONCEPT DEFINITION 2.0

1. BIO-INJECTION MECHANICS
Manifestation: **Ocular Overlay & Haptic Feedback Loop.**
Visual: Henoch is not just plugged in; he is forcefully synchronized. His eyes act as the render-viewport. We see the reflection of the simulation in his irises, but it's overlayed with a "DEBUG_MODE_ACTIVE" red tint. The skin on his face ripples as if wind-blown, but the air is still—this is phantom haptic data bleeding into his physical nerves.

2. MESH-BREAK PHYSICS
Phenomenon: **Runtime Asset Injection.**
Visual: The "Stars" (Payloads) do not interact with physics initially. They clip through trees and clouds without collision (Noclip Mode) until they trigger the "Execute" command at ground level. The "Pasture" does not shatter; it is overwritten. Grass textures are instantaneously replaced by flat grey "Developer Textures" in a radius around the impact, signifying resource reallocation to the intruder.

3. PACING STRATEGY
Rhythm: **The Denial of Service (DoS) Curve.**
Phase 1 (Latency): Slow, floating camera. 24fps. The system is under load but stable.
Phase 2 (Packet Storm): Rapid cutting. 12fps (simulated lag). The arrival of the masses causes frame-skips.
Phase 3 (Crash): Glitch-art aesthetic. Compression artifacts. The rendering engine can't keep up with the polygon count of the new entities.

4. AUDIO DESIGN
Ambience: **The Coil Whine.**
A high-pitched, piercing sine wave (15kHz) that modulates in pitch as the "Stars" fall, mimicking the sound of capacitors under extreme load.
Event Sounds:
- Grazing: The sound of hard drives scratching/seeking.
- Birth: The wet tearing of audio buffers (digital distortion) mixed with hydraulic pumps.

5. TRANSITIONS
Method: **LOD Popping.**
Scene changes occur not by fading, but by geometry instantly snapping from low-poly to high-poly, or the camera "teleporting" to a new coordinate instantly (0 frames travel time).

6. FRAMEWORK & TIMING
Total Duration: ~80 Seconds.
Clip Count: 17 Clips.
Logic: Breakdown of action into micro-events (Arrival -> Impact -> Reaction).

7. DIALOG CHECK
Status: **NEGATIVE.**
Reasoning: The entities are automated scripts. Communication is wireless/binary. Henoch is a muted observer.

8. SPATIAL CONTEXT (ARRIVAL)
Method: **Coordinate Shift.**
Visual: No travel. Henoch blinks. In frame A, he is in the "Server Room". In frame B (eye open), the "Pasture" is already rendered fully around him.
