[CONCEPT_TITLE]
PROTOCOL_EPOCH_91: THE_BOOT_SEQUENCE

[1. BIO-INJECTION_MECHANICS]
Technology: SUBCUTANEOUS_FIBER_OPTIC_IGNITION.
Manifestation: Enoch does not "glow" magically. Instead, his skin translucency increases to 80%, revealing a nervous system replaced by high-bandwidth fiber-optic cabling. As the "Spirit" (Root Access) loads, these cables ignite sequentially from the spine outward, pulsing with high-frequency white light (6500K). Heat vents on his neck open, releasing pressurized steam (coolant) to prevent thermal shutdown during the prophecy calculation.

[2. MESH-BREAK_DYNAMICS]
Phenomenon: TEXTURE_STREAMING_FAILURE.
Visuals: As Enoch processes the future (The Weeks), the local reality (Methuselah's Node) cannot maintain its render budget. High-fidelity stone textures degrade into flat, untextured grey polygons (LOD 0 -> LOD 5). Non-essential objects (furniture, background props) simply cull out of existence (pop-out) to free up RAM for the holographic projection of the prophecy.

[3. PACING_STRATEGY]
Rhythm: BUFFER_UNDERRUN_STUTTER.
A-Side (The Admin): Enoch's movements are interpolated at 120fps—unnaturally smooth and fast.
B-Side (The Users): Methuselah and the family move at a laggy 12fps, creating a visual disconnect that emphasizes Enoch's superior processing speed.
Cuts: No fades. Hard cuts only. Occasional "Freeze Frame" glitches where the video hangs for 0.2s before snapping to the next clip, simulating a processor struggling to keep up.

[4. AUDIO_DESIGN]
Soundscape: SERVER_ROOM_TURBINE.
Ambience: The base layer is the deafening roar of industrial cooling fans spinning up.
Voice: Enoch's voice is not flanged; it is synthesized. It consists of multiple audio tracks layered: a deep sub-bass carrier wave and a high-pitch data-screech (like a modem handshake) that modulates into intelligible speech.
Events: Every time a "Week" is revealed, a heavy "Bass Drop" (Sub-kick) hits, shaking the camera.

[5. TRANSITIONS]
Method: I-FRAME_DESTRUCTION (DATAMOSHING).
Visual: The pixels of the previous scene do not fade; they are "dragged" into the next scene using motion vector distortion. The colors of the "Violence" epoch smear into the "Flood" epoch like wet paint, simulating a corrupted video codec.

[6. SPATIAL_CONTEXT_&_ARRIVAL]
Location: NODE_SINAI_CORE (The Mainframe).
Arrival: Enoch is the Terminal. He does not arrive; he is activated. He stands in a docking bay, cables detaching from his spine. Methuselah and the family "spawn" in (materialize via blue wireframe assembly) as they are summoned to the instance.
