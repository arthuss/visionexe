CONCEPT: SYSTEM PURGE & KERNEL JUDGMENT (CHAPTER 62) - VERSION 2.0

1. BIO-INJECTION (THE COMPATIBILITY ERROR)
The "pain" described in Verse 4 is identified as **Legacy Firmware Incompatibility**. The "Kings" are running `GEN1_ARCH` (Flesh 1.0) which cannot process the bandwidth of the `PRIME_ADMIN` presence.
- Physical Manifestation: **Thermal Throttling**. The internal processors of the Kings overheat instantly. Their gold-plated skin acts as a heat sink that has reached saturation.
- Tissue Failure: The skin does not just "crack"; it delaminates. We see the synthetic mesh underneath glowing bright orange (`#FF4500`).
- Coolant Leak: The "sweat/tears" are rendered as boiling dielectric fluid (neon green) venting from tear ducts and pores to prevent total core meltdown.

2. MESH-BREAK (THE RENDER FAILURE)
The environment cannot handle the polygon count of the "Elect One" while simultaneously rendering the "Kings".
- LOD Popping: The floor geometry fluctuates between High-Poly (Reflective Obsidian) and Low-Poly (Wireframe Grid) in real-time.
- Texture Thrashing: The Kings' ornate armor textures fail to load, flashing fleetingly to "Checkered Pink/Black" default textures before re-loading, simulating VRAM exhaustion.
- Clipping: The Kings do not stand on the floor; their feet clip 5cm *into* the mesh, creating digital ripples where solids intersect.

3. PACING-STRATEGIE (LATENCY EDITING)
The rhythm visualizes the processing disparity.
- **The Admin (Elect One):** 120fps. Movement is interpolated, smooth, and predictable.
- **The Legacy Users (Kings):** 12fps (Stop-Motion feel). They move in jerky stutter-steps.
- **The Glitch-Cut:** The edit points occur on "Lag Spikes". We cut on the exact frame a King freezes or glitches, snapping instantly to the smooth perspective of the Throne.

4. AUDIO-DESIGN (THE SERVER ROOM)
- **Base:** The "Throne Room" hums with a 40Hz sub-bass, the sound of massive cooling fans moving air.
- **The Voice:** The "Word that Kills" is a directional **Ultrasonic Pulse**. We hear the *impact* (wet thuds, armor rattling) but not the voice itself. It is a frequency above human hearing that causes data corruption.
- **The Plea:** The Kings' voices are bit-crushed (8-bit), suffering from packet loss (stuttering syllables).

5. ÜBERGÄNGE (FORCE-LOAD)
- **Ingress:** No travel. The Kings are `SPAWNED` into the coordinate system `(0,0,0)`. They appear in a T-Pose for 1 frame before physics applies and they crumble under gravity.
- **Egress:** `DELETE_BY_VOXEL`. The Kings are not moved; their mesh topology is atomized into 1cm cubes which lose gravity and float upwards into the system intake.
