CONCEPT ANALYSIS: CHAPTER 101 - SYSTEM INTEGRITY AUDIT V2.0

1. BIO-INJECTION (DIAGNOSTIC OVERLAY)
Manifestation: HOLOGRAPHIC_DERMIS_DISPLAY.
Henoch does not merely project light; his skin surface acts as a `FLEXIBLE_OLED_CANVAS`.
Visual: His skin shader is `MATTE_CERAMIC_WHITE` (0% Roughness). When the audit begins, the ceramic becomes translucent. Beneath the surface, we do not see veins, but `LIQUID_COOLING_CHANNELS` filled with fluorescent orange coolant (indicating system heat/stress).
System Feedback: Error codes in Ge'ez are not floating in the air; they are burned directly into his iris textures, visible only when the camera is in Extreme Close-Up (`MACRO_LENS`). His blinks are mechanical shutter closures (`APERTURE_BLADES`).

2. MESH-BREAK (RENDER FAILURE)
Event: The "Shutting of Heaven" and "The Storm".
Visual: RESOURCE_STARVATION_ARTIFACTS.
The simulation is being throttled. The engine cannot load high-resolution assets in time.
- Phase 1 (The Sky): `SKYBOX_FAILURE`. The cloud textures fail to stream. The sky is replaced by a default `CHECKERBOARD_GRID` (Grey/White) that flickers in patches.
- Phase 2 (The Rain): `PHYSICS_SUSPENSION`. The rain does not stop naturally. The raindrops freeze in mid-air and turn into rigid silver spheres (`DEFAULT_MESH_SPHERE`), waiting for physics calculation cycles that never arrive.
- Phase 3 (The Ocean): `VERTEX_SPIKING`. The waves do not just turn to voxels; the vertices of the water mesh explode outward in infinite jagged spikes (`NON_MANIFOLD_GEOMETRY`), tearing through the hull of the ship.

3. PACING-STRATEGIE (LATENCY RHYTHM)
Style: BUFFERING_LAG.
The edit mimics a struggling GPU.
- 00s-20s (Packet Loss): The video stutters. We see Henoch turn his head, then the frame freezes for 0.5s, then snaps to the end of the movement (`FRAME_SKIP`).
- 20s-50s (Rubber-Banding): The "Kings of Ships" sequence. The ship crashes into a wave, then instantly snaps back 2 seconds in time and crashes again, looping 3 times in rapid succession (`NETWORK_LAG_LOOP`).
- 50s-65s (Crash to Desktop): The chaotic movement ends abruptly in a single frame of black, followed by the silent, static "Sandbox Boundary" scene.

4. AUDIO-DESIGN (HARDWARE STRESS)
Ambient: `DATA_CENTER_DRONE`. A low-frequency hum (50Hz) layered with the sound of thousands of hard drive read/write heads clicking frantically (`SEEK_NOISE`).
Events: The "Roar of the Sea" is replaced by `DIGITAL_DISTORTION_SCREAM` (Bitcrushed White Noise).
Voice: Henoch’s voice is dry, stripped of reverb. It sounds like a generic Text-to-Speech synthesis that occasionally glitches on a syllable ("The w-w-w-water is bound").

5. ÜBERGÄNGE (LOD_POPPING)
Transition Logic: ASSET_SWAP.
No travel. The camera stays fixed. The world around Henoch changes instantly.
Visual: Henoch stands in the center of the frame. In Frame A, he is in the arid desert. In Frame B, without him moving a pixel, the desert is replaced by the deck of the ship. The lighting changes instantly from "Harsh Sun" to "Storm Strobe". It is a `LEVEL_LOAD_INSTANT` event.
