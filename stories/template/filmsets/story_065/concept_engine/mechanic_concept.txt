[SECTION 1: TECH_MECHANICS_&_AESTHETICS]

1. BIO-INJECTION (ENTITY_STATE):
Henoch (Admin Instance): No biological processes. He is a "Hard-Light Construct".
Visuals: Skin is semi-translucent silicate (`MAT_FrostedGlass`), revealing an internal skeleton of gold circuitry (`RIG_Goldconductor`). Hair is fiber-optic cabling carrying live data streams (white light pulsing). Eyes are mechanical apertures (`f/0.95`) with no pupils, projecting lidar scan grids.

2. MESH-BREAK (THE TILT):
The "Tilting Earth" is a `Global_Transform_Error`. The XYZ coordinate system of the "Earth" object rotates relative to the "Skybox".
Visuals: The horizon line rotates 45 degrees, but the user gravity vector remains local. This causes "Screen Tearing" in the sky texture. Mountains lose collision meshes (`noclip` enabled) and objects sink into them. The "Void" (Null Black) bleeds through the seams of the terrain chunks.

3. PACING-STRATEGIE (LATENCY_CURVE):
Phase 1 (The Glitch): Low framerate simulation (12fps). Dropped frames. Jump cuts where Noah "teleports" forward due to lag.
Phase 2 (The Admin): Instant transition to 120fps fluid motion upon Henoch's arrival. Zero motion blur. Perfect stabilization.
Phase 3 (The Data): Montages of holographic data displays overlaying the reality, fast UI interactions contrasting with static character blocking.

4. AUDIO-DESIGN (THE_NOISE_FLOOR):
Environment: "Digital Shearing" - the sound of heavy metal dragging on concrete, combined with HDD read/write scratching.
Voice (Noah): Audio buffer underrun. Stuttering, robotic artifacting, bit-crushed.
Voice (Henoch): Direct Neural Link. No acoustic properties. Sound is dry, centered, immediate.
Ambiance: Rising "Shepard Tone" (infinite rising pitch) representing the increasing system instability.

5. SPATIAL CONTEXT (THE_BOUNDARY):
Noah reaches the limit of the rendered zone.
Visuals: High-res textures abruptly transition to "Developer Textures" (Grey Grid), then to wireframe, then to void.
Arrival: Henoch does not fly. He executes `SPAWN_AT_CURSOR`. A vertical "Data Beam" (The Uplink) pierces the skybox, striking the grid. He extrudes from this beam like a 3D print resolving in real-time.
