1. BIO-INJECTION (THE OBSERVER)
Henoch’s biological interface is overridden by `DAEMON_THREAD_VIZ`. The optic nerve is not merely overlaying data but actively culling "irrelevant" render data. His perception creates a `TUNNEL_VISION_ARTIFACT` where peripheral vision dissolves into static to prioritize the active scan lines of the Architects. Physical sensation is reduced to a rhythmic `HAPTIC_THROB` at the base of the skull, synchronized with the server clock ticks (60Hz).

2. MESH-BREAK (THE MEASUREMENT)
The "Measurement" is strictly `GEOMETRY_DECONSTRUCTION`. The `QUANTUM_CALIPERS` do not just measure distance; they verify data integrity against a checksum.
- The Breach: As the laser-cords sweep the terrain, the "Texture Map" (Snow/Ice) is forcibly unloaded.
- The Core View: We see the `RAW_VERTEX_DATA`. Corrupted sectors appear as black voids or red "Null Reference" jagged spikes. Valid sectors glow in stable `GREEN_HEX` lattices. The physical world is revealed as a fragile skin over a mathematical skeleton.

3. PACING-STRATEGIE (THE RHYTHM)
Phase 1 (The Scan): Clinical, staccato editing. 2-second clips. Scan -> Result -> Log.
Phase 2 (The Query): Slower, investigative. Henoch interrupts the flow to question the process. The editing breathes here to allow dialogue focus.
Phase 3 (The Recovery): Fluid, ascending motion. As data is pulled from the "Sea" and "Desert", camera movements become vertical and sweeping, matching the "uplink" of restored souls.

4. AUDIO-DESIGN (THE FREQUENCY)
- The North: High-velocity wind noise mixed with the whine of cooling fans (`CRYOGENIC_ACOUSTICS`).
- The Cords: A distinct "Zippering" sound, like a high-tension cable snapping, followed by a digital "Handshake" chirp.
- The Synchronization: A chaotic dissonance of thousands of independent frequencies slowly sliding into a perfect, deafening `UNISON_SINE_WAVE` (The Unified Word).

5. ÜBERGÄNGE (THE SCAN)
Transitions are driven by `DATAMOSH_WIPES`. The frame freezes for a microsecond, pixelates into compression artifacts, and resolves into the next angle. This simulates a high-bandwidth video stream struggling to keep up with the rendering engine changes.

6. FRAMEWORK & TIMING
Total Duration: ~90 Seconds.
Clip Count: 18 Clips (Expanded for Dialogue and Recovery Sequence).
- Clips 01-04: Deployment of Scan Units (Angels) to Sector North.
- Clips 05-08: Henoch’s Query and Uriel’s Explanation (Shot-Reverse-Shot).
- Clips 09-12: The Deep Recovery (Sea & Desert scanning).
- Clips 13-15: The Elect One (Admin) authorizing the restore.
- Clips 16-18: System Synchronization and Stabilization.

7. DIALOG-CHECK
REQUIRED. The interaction defines the context of the mechanical action.
- Dialogue: "Why have they taken those cords?" / "They have gone to measure."
- Execution: Close-up lip-sync is critical. The "Cords" must be visible in the background of Henoch’s shot to maintain spatial continuity.

8. SPATIAL CONTEXT (ARRIVAL)
Henoch arrives via `NODE_HOPPING`.
- Visual: He takes a step in a previous scene (e.g., Garden), the air shatters like glass, and his foot lands on the obsidian surface of the North. No flight. Immediate displacement.
- Platform: He stands on the `OBSIDIAN_DISC` (Levitation Platform) drifting 10 meters above the Data-Ice.
