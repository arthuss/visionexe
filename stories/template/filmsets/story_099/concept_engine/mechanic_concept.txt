CONCEPT_ANALYSIS_CHAPTER_99_V2

1. BIO-INJECTION (The Auditor):
Henoch functions as a "Runtime Diagnostic Tool". He is not fully rendered in this partition to save resources. He appears as a "Wireframe Spectre" – a translucent, glowing blue mesh of a human form, oscillating between 30% and 70% opacity. His "eyes" are projected frustums (camera viewcones) visible in the fog. He moves via "Transform Snap" – no walking animation, just coordinate updates.

2. MESH-BREAK (The Corruption):
The "Sinners" (Corrupted Nodes) suffer from "Normal Map Inversion". Their surfaces look inside-out or deeply pitted where they should be smooth. The environment exhibits "Z-Fighting" on a massive scale: buildings flicker rapidly between two contradictory architectural styles (e.g., mud brick vs. rusted steel) because the system cannot decide which texture has priority.

3. PACING-STRATEGIE (The Frame Drop):
The pacing mimics a struggling GPU.
- Corrupted sequences: Irregular frame pacing (stutter). We cut on the "wrong" beat to simulate lag.
- Righteous sequences: V-Sync enabled. Perfectly smooth, locked 60fps, utilizing slow, deliberate dolly moves to emphasize stability.

4. AUDIO-DESIGN (The Coil Whine):
- Sinners: The sound of a cooling fan with a broken bearing (grinding drone). High-frequency capacitor squeal.
- Idols: A loop of corrupt audio buffer (stuttering "glitch" noise).
- Righteous: A perfectly flat noise floor (silence) punctuated by soft, harmonic "chimes" indicating successful data packets.

5. ÜBERGÄNGE (The Draw Call):
We use "LOD Popping" as a transition. A scene switches from Low-Poly (Blockout) to High-Poly (Final Render) instantly to signify a change in focus or realization.

6. FRAMEWORK & TIMING:
Total Duration: ~70 Seconds.
Clip Count: 16 Clips.
Reasoning: The "Process Kill" (Mother/Child) and "Idolatry" sequences require multi-shot breakdowns (Setup -> Action -> Result) to convey the mechanical horror without dialogue.

7. DIALOG-CHECK:
No verbal dialogue. Text overlays in Ge'ez appear as "System Warnings" in the air. The "Sinners" mouth words, but no sound comes out (Audio Driver Failure).

8. SPATIAL CONTEXT (The Node Hop):
Henoch enters via "Asset Streaming". He doesn't walk in; he loads in. First his bounding box appears, then his wireframe, then his textures. The world builds around him chunk by chunk.
