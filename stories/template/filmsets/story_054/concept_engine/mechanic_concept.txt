CONCEPT_CORE: SYSTEM_PURGE_AND_COOLANT_FLUSH
The "Deep Valley" is interpreted as the primary Heat Sink and Garbage Collection Sector of the simulation. The "Fire" is the thermal exhaust from processing corrupted entities (Kings/Mighty). The "Chains" are physical containment protocols (Magnetic Locking Mechanisms). The "Waters" represent a massive Coolant Flush (System Reset) initiated to sterilize the infected sectors.

1. BIO-INJECTION
Enoch's ocular sensors shift to `THERMAL_SPECTRAL_MODE` (High Contrast Orange/Blue) to perceive the heat signatures in the valley without optical damage. His auditory receptors dampen by 80% to filter the deafening roar of the thermal exhaust. A HUD overlay `THREAT_ANALYSIS_V2` highlights the "Kings and Mighty" with red bounding boxes labeled `CORRUPTED_ENTITY`.

2. MESH-BREAK
The "Deep Valley" is not natural terrain but a `GEOMETRY_TEAR`. The texture of the earth peels back like burnt skin, revealing the raw, glowing wireframe and the "burning" core of the server architecture underneath. The "Kings" do not walk; they glitch and clip through the floor, dragged by invisible collision vectors toward the heat sink.

3. PACING-STRATEGIE
Phase 1 (The Valley): Slow, heavy, industrial rhythm. The movement of the "chains" is mechanical and rhythmic (clank-hiss-clank).
Phase 2 (The Dialogue): Static, stable camera for data transfer between Enoch and the Angel.
Phase 3 (The Flush): Accelerating chaos. The release of the waters is sudden and violent, shifting from solid mechanics to fluid dynamics.

4. AUDIO-DESIGN
No choirs.
The Valley: `HEAVY_SERVER_ROAR` (thousands of cooling fans at max RPM) mixed with `ELECTRICAL_ARCING`.
The Chains: `HYDRAULIC_PRESS` sounds, metallic grinding, magnetic locking thuds.
The Waters: `HIGH_PRESSURE_VALVE_RELEASE`, the sound of rushing liquid in pipes, not a natural river.

5. ÜBERGÄNGE
From Normal to Valley: A "LOD_DROP" (Level of Detail Drop). The realistic desert floor dissolves into low-poly geometry, then into the burning void.
From Furnace to Flood: A "SYSTEM_ALERT" flash (Red to Blue strobe). The heat turns to steam instantly as the coolant hits.

6. FRAMEWORK & TIMING (60 Seconds / 15-18 Clips)
We need extended clip counts for the dialogue and the complex multi-stage event (Chains -> Furnace -> Flood).

7. DIALOG-CHECK
YES. Enoch interrogates the Angel of Peace regarding the specific function of the "Iron Fetters".
Shot-Reverse-Shot required:
- Shot A: Enoch (MCU) pointing/looking at the chains.
- Shot B: Angel of Peace (MCU) responding, gesturing to the valley.
- Shot C: Insert Shot of the Chains (POV).

8. SPATIAL CONTEXT (ARRIVAL)
Enoch is positioned on a "Gantry" or "Observation Deck" - a floating platform of obsidian glass hovering directly above the Heat Sink Valley. No travel needed; he is observing the processing plant from above.
