CONCEPT_CORE: SYSTEM_PURGE_AND_COOLANT_FLUSH_V2
The "Deep Valley" is identified as the Central Thermal Exhaust Port (`/dev/null` equivalent for high-priority threads). The "Fire" is not combustion, but the visible thermal radiation from massive data-deletion cycles. The "Chains" are `CRYPTOGRAPHIC_HASH_LOCKS`—immutable logic constraints with zero physical mass. The "Waters" are a dielectric coolant flood used to execute a hard system reset.

1. BIO-INJECTION
Enoch does not simply "look." His optical sub-routines switch to `THERMAL_GRADIENT_VISUALIZATION`. His skin shader shifts from "Parian Marble" to "Ceramic Heat-Shielding" (Matte Grey/White) to reflect the intense radiation of the valley. He is not feeling heat; he is reading temperature data.

2. MESH-BREAK
The "Deep Valley" geometry is unstable. The polygon count in the pit is so low (Level of Detail 0) that the "Kings" fall through the textures. The "burning" is z-fighting textures and vertex displacement errors caused by the system overload. The "earth" doesn't crack; the topology tears, revealing the void (skybox) underneath.

3. PACING-STRATEGIE
Phase 1 (The Lock): Precision, surgical, snapping. 24fps.
Phase 2 (The Query): Static, low-angle, monumental. 24fps.
Phase 3 (The Flush): Exponential acceleration. Frame rate ramps from 24fps to 60fps distinct look to simulate fluid simulation speed.

4. AUDIO-DESIGN
The Valley: A Shepard Tone (illusion of infinitely rising pitch) combined with the sound of a massive server farm ventilation system.
The Chains: Not clanking metal. The sound of a high-voltage capacitor charging and the "click" of a server rack locking into place.
The Waters: The sound of nitrogen release (hissing) and heavy liquid impact (viscous fluid), distinct from natural water.

5. ÜBERGÄNGE
From Normal to Valley: A `WIREFRAME_SCANLINE` effect passes over the terrain. Where the scanline passes, the desert texture is stripped away, leaving the raw industrial heat-sink architecture exposed.

6. FRAMEWORK & TIMING
15 Clips. Total Runtime: ~70 Seconds.
Expanded sequence for the Dialogue (Clips 5-7) and the dual-source coolant injection (Clips 11-13).

7. DIALOG-CHECK
YES. Enoch (The User) queries the interface (Angel of Peace) about the function of the sub-routines (Chains).
Shot-Reverse-Shot implemented:
- Shot A: Enoch (Over-Shoulder) -> Looking at Chains.
- Shot B: Angel (POV) -> Responding.
- Shot C: Detail of the specific "Hosts" being targeted.

8. SPATIAL CONTEXT (ARRIVAL)
Enoch is instantiated on the "Gantry" (Observation Bridge 01). No travel animation needed. He manifests via `NODE_TRANSLOCATION` (a vertical light-slice) directly onto the black glass walkway overlooking the heat sink.
