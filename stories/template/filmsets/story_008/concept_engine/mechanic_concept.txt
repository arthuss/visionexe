CONCEPT: PROTOCOL_UNBOUND // THE UNAUTHORIZED UPDATE // VERSION 2.0

### ANALYSIS OF LEGACY DRAFT
1.  **Magical Drift:** The "liquid silver" and "plasma" elements leaned too heavily on fantasy tropes. Version 2.0 must replace "morphing" with "compiling". Armor shouldn't flow; it should snap into place via geometry instancing.
2.  **Lack of Process:** The old draft showed results (sword exists) but not the mechanism (sword rendering). We need to show the *load* on the system.
3.  **Audio:** Needs to be more aggressive. Less "server hum", more "bandwidth saturation screech".

### CONCEPT V2.0 SPECIFICATIONS

1.  **BIO-INJECTION (The Antimony Patch)**
    *   **Mechanism:** Not liquid makeup. It is a **Hardware-Interfacing Ocular Implant**. A micro-injector (needle-drive) pierces the caruncle (corner of the eye) to sever the optic nerve connection to the default simulation and splice in a "Filter-Overlay".
    *   **Visual:** The white of the eye turns black (sclera tattoo style) not from ink, but from the cooling fluid required for the overclocked retina. The iris becomes a spinning aperture shutter.

2.  **MESH-BREAK (The Crafting Glitch)**
    *   **Mechanism:** When humans craft weapons (objects not in the original item database), the physics engine cannot resolve the collision meshes.
    *   **Visual:** **Z-Fighting**. The sword flickers rapidly between being visible and invisible. The air around the blade shows "Texture Tears" – jagged black triangles where the void behind the skybox is visible. Sparks are not fire, they are **Dead Pixels** falling like snow.

3.  **PACING STRATEGY (The Upload Bar)**
    *   **Phase 1: Handshake (0-15s):** Slow, clinical macro shots of the tools (The Needle, The Raw Code). Establishing the connection.
    *   **Phase 2: Data Transfer (15-45s):** A rapid A/B editing pattern. [Teacher Action] -> [Human Reaction/Glitch]. The editing speed increases as the "Download" percentage rises.
    *   **Phase 3: Kernel Panic (45-60s):** System instability. Visual tearing. The frame itself seems to de-sync.

4.  **AUDIO-DESIGN**
    *   **Primary Layer:** A Shepard Tone (illusion of infinitely rising pitch) representing the rising system entropy.
    *   **Impact Sounds:** Not metal on metal. The sound of a **Corrupted Audio File** (digital screech/static) triggers every time a weapon hits or an injection finishes.
    *   **The Cry:** A multi-layered burst of modem-handshake tones screaming in unison, ending in a sudden, deafening silence (Connection Cut).

5.  **TRANSITIONS**
    *   **The Buffer-Underrun:** The image freezes for 2 frames, pixels drag (datamosh), then snaps to the next scene. Simulating lag.

6.  **SPATIAL CONTEXT (Node: AZAZEL_CACHE)**
    *   **Location:** A hidden partition. A "Debug Room". The floor is a grid texture (checkerboard) that hasn't fully loaded (greyboxing). The lighting is purely artificial—no sun, only the emission from the Watchers and the raw assets.
