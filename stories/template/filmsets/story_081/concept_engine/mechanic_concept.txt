1. BIO-INJECTION MECHANICS (THE READ)
The "Heavenly Tablets" are defined as a `HOVERING_DATA_CLUSTER`. Interaction is strictly non-tactile. Data transfer occurs via high-bandwidth `OPTICAL_DATA_INJECTION`.
- Physical Manifestation: Enoch’s `SOLAR_APERTURE_EYES` do not project; they act as receivers for high-intensity blue laser streams.
- Tissue Reaction: The orbital sockets exhibit `SUB_DERMAL_HEATING` (reddish subsurface scattering indicating CPU load). Temporal veins pulse in sync with the bit-rate.
- Neural-Feedback: The C1-Atlas neural port vents excess thermal energy as localized steam jets to prevent cognitive core meltdown.

2. MESH-BREAK PHYSICS (THE DROP)
The transition from `DIMENSION_SERVER_PRIME` to `LOCAL_EARTH_CACHE` is a controlled `LOD_DEGRADATION_EVENT`.
- Geometry Behavior: The perfect Euclidean planes of the Server Hall do not shatter; they undergo step-wise retopology. High-Poly chrome surfaces simplify into Low-Poly wireframes, then voxelize, and finally bake into organic mud/clay textures.
- Texture Mapping: Clean specular maps are aggressively overwritten by high-frequency noise maps (sand/dust).
- Fluid Dynamics: Atmospheric density shifts instantly from sterile vacuum (zero drag) to particulate-heavy desert heat (high drag).

3. PACING & RHYTHM STRATEGY
- Phase 1 (Ingest): 120bpm. Rapid-fire cuts. Macro photography. Stroboscopic lighting syncs with data packets.
- Phase 2 (Admin Protocol): 60bpm. Dead-center symmetry. Camera movements are purely linear (dolly/track). The "Saints" are static monoliths.
- Phase 3 (Re-Entry): Glitch-Rhythm. Asynchronous edits. Intentional dropped frames to simulate the kinetic impact of rematerialization.

4. AUDIO ARCHITECTURE
- Primary Ambience: `50HZ_SERVER_HUM` (Omnipresent in Phase 1).
- Data Transfer: `HDD_WRITE_CLICKS` (High-frequency, insectoid chirping) synced to eye movement.
- Vocal Tracks: The Three Holy Ones do not speak organically. Their communication is a `MULTI_CHANNEL_HARMONIC_SYNTH` chord.
- Re-Entry Event: A violent `THUNDERCRACK` (Air displacement) followed immediately by absolute silence, then gradual fade-in of wind.

5. TRANSITION MECHANIC (VECTOR_SCAN)
The dimensional shift is visualized as a vertical `RASTER_SCAN`. A hyper-bright horizontal scanline sweeps from top to bottom.
- Above Line: Server Prime (Gold/White/Clean).
- Below Line: Earth Sector (Ocher/Dust/Dirty).
The line acts as a boolean eraser/creator brush.

6. SPATIAL CONTEXT (ARRIVAL AT NODE_0)
- Location: `SECTOR_METHUSELAH_HOME`. A primitive mud-brick habitat (LOD 0) in a barren landscape.
- Arrival Logic: No flight trajectory. `INSTANT_MESH_LOAD`. Enoch spawns in T-Pose for 0.2s before blending into `IDLE_STANCE_01`.
- Physics: A radial dust shockwave expands from the spawn coordinates.
