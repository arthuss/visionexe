MECHANICS_PROTOCOL:
1. BIO-INJECTION: CHEM_SENSORY_DATA_STREAM. The "scent" of nard, cinnamon, and pepper is not biological. It is interpreted by Enoch's suit sensors as specific chemical coolants leaking from the high-performance infrastructure. This data is fed directly into his neural cortex via the collar-tubes, visualized as spectral analysis graphs overlaying his vision (Amber/Cinnamon, Green/Pepper, Violet/Nard).
2. MESH-BREAK: THE_FIREWALL_BOUNDARY. The "Red Sea" is a visual metaphor for the primary firewall separating the chaotic "Wasteland" partition from the "Garden" (Kernel) partition. Crossing it causes a "V-Sync" tear in reality—the horizon splits, pixels smear horizontally, and the color grading snaps instantly from dusty red to sterile, clean laboratory white.
3. PACING-STRATEGIE: VELOCITY_TO_PRECISION.
   - Phase 1 (Traverse): Mach-speed aerial survey. Linear, aggressive motion.
   - Phase 2 (Infiltration): Sudden deceleration upon passing Zotiel.
   - Phase 3 (Analysis): Static, locked-off camera angles. Micro-movements only. Reflects the stability of the Root environment.
4. AUDIO-DESIGN: THE_DATA_CENTER. No birds, no wind. The ambient sound is a deafening silence overlaid with a 40Hz sub-bass hum (server idle state) and the rhythmic "woosh" of massive nitrogen cooling vents (the "fragrance").
5. TRANSITIONS: LENS_SWAP_GLITCH. Switching perspectives (Enoch POV to 3rd Person) is handled via a digital iris-wipe effect, simulating a change in surveillance camera feeds.
6. SPATIAL CONTEXT: MERKABAH_DISENGAGE. Enoch approaches in the rotating Tetrahedron field (Flight Mode). Upon passing Zotiel (The Gatekeeper), the field collapses into his suit geometry, dropping him onto the "circuit-board" floor of the Garden.
