CONCEPT: PROTOCOL_15_DAEMON_ORIGIN V2.0

1. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & MECHANICS
The "Voice" is identified not as an acoustic wave, but as a Kernel-Level Firmware Update delivered via direct neural injection. The interaction defines the distinction between "Kernel-Space" (Watchers/Teguha) and "User-Space" (Humans).
BIO-INJECTION: The data transfer causes thermal throttling in Enoch's biological avatar.
Manifestation: Subdermal circuitry (The Blue Trace) overheats, turning from cool cyan to warning orange.
Ocular: The Solar Apertures do not just glow; they project the incoming code-stream outwards, turning Enoch into a projector for the simulation.
Motor Control: Involuntary galvanic muscle response. The neck snaps back rigidly as the bandwidth peaks.

2. VISUAL DIALECTIC (THE MESH-BREAK)
The sequence visualizes the ontological error of the "Fall".
High Heaven: Rendered as procedural, non-euclidean fractal geometry. Zero entropy. Perfect math.
Earth (Flesh): Rendered as stochastic, noisy organic photogrammetry. High entropy.
The Sin (Hybridization): A forced Boolean Union of fractal math and organic mesh.
Result: The "Giants" are not biological monsters, but rendering errors. Massive Z-Fighting, vertex explosions, and texture stretching. They are "glitched" assets that the physics engine cannot resolve.

3. EDITING & PACING STRATEGY
Total Clips: 17
Total Duration: ~75 Seconds
Phase A (The Upload): Clips 1-3. High stability, locked-off camera. Heavy, industrial atmosphere.
Phase B (The Error Log): Clips 4-9. Rapid, rhythmic cuts. Glitch transitions. High visual noise. Use of "Datamosh" to transition between the ideal and the corrupt.
Phase C (The Daemon): Clips 10-12. Handheld, floating camera. Uncanny valley movement.
Phase D (The Protocol): Clips 13-17. Return to stability. Cold, decisive framing.

4. AUDIO ENGINEERING
Ambience: A 40Hz server-room drone, layering up into a screaming modem-handshake pitch during the "Giants" visualization.
The Voice: No human speech. A granular synthesis chord that vibrates the low-end frequencies.
Foley:
- The "Giants" sound like digital clipping (square waves).
- The "Spirits" sound like high-voltage static discharge.
- Enoch's movement sounds like hydraulic fluid in motion (Liquid Linen).

5. SPATIAL CONTEXT
Enoch is located in the "Crystal Sanctum" (The Throne Room/Server Core).
The walls of the room dim to black, effectively becoming a 360-degree Holodeck.
The "Earth History" is projected around him as floating, holographic windows that he observes from the center.
