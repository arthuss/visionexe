CONCEPT: PROTOCOL_15_DAEMON_ORIGIN

1. BIO-INJECTION MECHANICS
The communication from the "Voice" is not acoustic. It is a direct kernel-level write operation to Enoch's auditory cortex.
Visual Manifestation: Enoch's eyes (Solar Apertures) dilate rapidly, pulsing with the rhythm of the incoming data stream.
Physical Reaction: Involuntary muscle spasms in the neck and jaw (The "shudder" of high-bandwidth data transfer).
Skin Surface: The "blue micro-circuitry" on his neck flares white-hot as the message is decrypted. He is not just hearing; he is being flashed with a new firmware update containing the judgment logic.

2. MESH-BREAK & GEOMETRY
As the Voice explains the nature of the Watchers (Spirit vs. Flesh), the rendering of the world shifts to "Debug View".
The "High Heaven" is visualized as perfect, non-euclidean geometric fractals (Zero Entropy).
The "Earth/Flesh" is visualized as chaotic, noisy organic meshes (High Entropy).
The "Sin" of the Watchers is shown as the forced boolean union of these two incompatible geometries, resulting in "Giants" – massive, unstable polygon structures that constantly clip and tear at the seams.

3. PACING & EDITING STRATEGY
Total Duration: 70 Seconds (Extended for complex exposition).
Rhythm:
00-15s (The Command): Static, heavy shots. High stability.
15-40s (The Analysis/Flashback): Rapid, rhythmic cuts synchronized with the data stream. A montage of "System Errors".
40-70s (The Consequence/Evil Spirits): Handheld, slightly shaky camera simulation to represent the instability introduced by the spirits.
Clip Count: 14 Clips.

4. AUDIO DESIGN
Primary Track: A deep, resonant "Server Hum" (40Hz) that modulates pitch based on the intensity of the message.
Voice Replacement: No human speech. The "Voice" is represented by a complex, multi-layered harmonic chord that vibrates the viewer's sub-woofer.
Foley: Digital tearing sounds (Datamosh) whenever the "Giants" or "Evil Spirits" are mentioned. The sound of high-voltage arcing.

5. SPATIAL CONTEXT
Enoch remains in the "Throne Room" (Crystal House) context from Chapter 14, but the walls have become transparent screens displaying the data.
He is the user standing in the center of a Holodeck simulation of the Earth's history.
