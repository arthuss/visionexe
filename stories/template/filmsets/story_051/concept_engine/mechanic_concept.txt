1. BIO-INJECTION MECHANICS
The resurrection of the dead is technically defined as `BATCH_PROFILE_RECOVERY` from the `SHEOL_COLD_STORAGE` partition.
- **Voxel-Assembly:** Subjects do not "rise" biologically; they are compiled. We see point-clouds (Lidar-Scan aesthetics, Cyan dots) aggregating in the void.
- **Mesh-Baking:** The "skin" is a procedural shader applied in real-time. We see the transition from wireframe -> untextured grey clay -> subsurface scattering skin.
- **Integrity Check:** A glowing `HASH_VERIFICATION_RINGS` (Gold) scans the compiled body from head to toe. If the checksum matches (The Righteous), the texture locks. If not, the mesh dissolves back into noise (static).

2. MESH-BREAK DYNAMICS
The "mountains leaping like rams" is a `PHYSICS_ENGINE_CALIBRATION_EVENT` or a `Z-BUFFER_CONFLICT`.
- **Vertex-Jitter:** The height-map of the terrain is unstable during the system reboot. Mountains snap vertically (translate Y-axis) in rigid, jerky increments, mimicking the "hopping" motion.
- **Polygon-Pop:** Distant geometry fails to render correctly, flickering between LOD0 (high detail) and LOD5 (blocky low-poly), creating a visual "stutter" in the landscape.
- **Texture-Stretching:** As a mountain "leaps," the rock textures momentarily stretch like melted plastic before snapping back, revealing the wireframe grid underneath.
