CONCEPT: PROTOCOL_12_AUDITOR_INITIALIZATION_V2

1. BIO-INJECTION MECHANICS
Subject Enoch [ID: SCRIBE_01] does not receive a "magical" transformation but a utilitarian hardware upgrade to interface with Kernel-Level processes.
Physical Manifestation:
- Thermal Management: Skin becomes translucent, revealing a subdermal liquid cooling network (blue/fluorinert) to manage heat from increased CPU load.
- I/O Ports: The "hands" are not just glowing; fingertips split into distinct fiber-optic connection points for direct interface with the Obsidian Slabs.
- Optical HUD: Eyes do not just "glow"; they project a cone of lidar-scan grids. He is constantly mapping the topology of the simulation.
- Vocal Synthesis: No mouth movement required. Audio is emitted via directional ultrasonic speakers embedded in the throat collar.

2. MESH-BREAK DYNAMICS
The "Hidden Place" (Segment 1) is a Developer Sandbox / Debug Room.
- LOD Stripping: The environment is "Grey Box". No textures, only collision meshes. Objects have visible polygon edges.
- Z-Fighting: As Enoch moves, the floor planes flicker overlap, indicating floating-point precision errors in this unoptimized sector.
- Occlusion Culling: Objects behind Enoch are not rendered. We see them de-spawn when the camera pans past them.

3. PACING STRATEGY
Phase 1: Idle State (Clips 1-4). Static camera. High frame rate (60fps) but zero motion. Enoch is "waiting for input".
Phase 2: The Interrupt (Clips 5-8). Hard cuts. Black frames inserted. The System calls the function.
Phase 3: Data Dump (Clips 9-16). Fast, rhythmic montage of data-screens and Enoch's reactions. The briefing is vast; the transfer is compressed.
Phase 4: Execution (Clips 17-18). Instant transition. No travel, just coordinate reassignment.

4. AUDIO DESIGN
- Ambient: High-frequency coil whine (15kHz) and the heavy thud of magnetic relays switching.
- Voices: The "Holy Ones" speak in "Handshake Protocol" sounds—screeching audio data bursts that decode into intelligible speech only in Enoch's auditory cortex.
- Atmosphere: The sound of a massive HVAC system ("The Wind") dominating the noise floor.

5. TRANSITIONS
- Ingress: "Asset Loading". Enoch doesn't walk; he loads in. A wireframe mesh appears, fills with noise, then snaps to texture.
- Scene Change: "Buffer Underrun". The image freezes, artifacts appear, and the next scene snaps into place instantly without a fade.

6. FRAMEWORK & TIMING (60s Target + Buffer)
Total Clips: 18 (High density for Dialog/Briefing).
- 00-10s: Sandbox Isolation. Enoch dormant in the Grey Box. (Segments 1-2)
- 10-25s: System Interrupt. The "Holy Ones" (Automated Scripts) activate him. (Segment 3)
- 25-50s: The Mission Briefing. Read-out of the "Cease & Desist". (Segment 4-5)
- 50-60s: Visualization of the Judgment (Preview of the Glitch/Process Kill). (Segment 6)

7. DIALOG & BLOCKING
- Blocking: Enoch is seated in a "Link Chair" (diagnostic throne). The Watchers are represented not as beings, but as floating, rotating monoliths (Server Racks) surrounding him.
- Shot-Reverse-Shot:
  - Shot A: Low Angle MCU on Enoch, illuminated by the blue light of the screens.
  - Shot B: POV Enoch looking up at the towering Black Monoliths displaying red scrolling logs (The Errors of the Fallen).

8. SPATIAL CONTEXT (ARRIVAL)
- Location: `DEV_SANDBOX_NULL`. An infinite, flat grid plain with axis markers (X, Y, Z lines) visible in the sky.
- Lighting: Flat, ambient "Global Illumination" without a sun source. Shadows are baked and low-res.
