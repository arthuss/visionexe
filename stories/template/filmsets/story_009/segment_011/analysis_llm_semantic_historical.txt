፡​ደመ፡​ወዓመፃ` (wä-bä-zə mälʾät kʷəlla mədr dämä wä-ʿamäṣa): "and thereby the whole earth was filled with blood and lawlessness". `በዝ` (bä-zə) means "by this", "through this". `ደመ፡​ወዓመፃ` are accusatives of respect/content: "filled *with respect to* blood and lawlessness". Standard syntax.
