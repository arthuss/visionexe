CONCEPT_NAME: NORTH_NODE_THERMAL_EXCHANGE_MATRIX_V2.0

1. BIO-INJECTION: THERMAL_REGULATION_OVERDRIVE.
To survive the absolute zero of the cooling exhaust, Henoch's biological shell enters an emergency overclock state.
*   Visual: His standard marble skin loses opacity, becoming semi-transparent frosted glass. Underneath, we see the vein-structure not as blood, but as glowing white coolant lines pumping at high velocity.
*   Physics: Frost accumulates on his eyelashes and tunic instantly, then sublimates (vaporizes) as his internal heat spikes.

2. MESH-BREAK: RENDER_HORIZON_CULLING.
The "Ends of the Earth" are defined as the limit of the active draw distance.
*   Visual: The terrain does not taper off naturally. It ends in a hard, jagged geometric edge. Beyond this edge is the NULL_VOID (pure alpha channel blackness).
*   Glitch: Floating un-rendered geometric primitives (white wireframe cubes) drift in the void, waiting for data assignment.

3. PACING-STRATEGIE: INDUSTRIAL_HYDRAULIC.
The rhythm mimics heavy machinery failing under load.
*   Phase 1 (0-15s): Heavy, grinding mechanical movement (The Vents opening). Slow, deliberate.
*   Phase 2 (15-30s): The Hiss. High-pressure release. Steady tension.
*   Phase 3 (30-60s): Cavitation. The system destablizes. Rapid, chaotic cuts matching the stuttering of the broken valves.

4. AUDIO-DESIGN: CRYOGENIC_VENTING.
No choirs. Pure industrial noise.
*   Primary: The deafening shriek of high-pressure gas escaping a narrow aperture (similar to a jet engine bleed valve).
*   Secondary: The "Clack-Clack-Clack" of the failed valves trying to close and reopening violently.
*   Impact: The Hail sounds like breaking glass and digital static bursts.

5. ÜBERGÄNGE: LOD_STREAMING_LAG.
Transitioning from the previous warm biome to the North Node.
*   Visual: The environment loads in "Level of Detail" stages. First, grey-box geometry blocks appearing around Henoch. Then, the high-res ice textures snap into existence 0.2s later. It emphasizes that the world is being generated on-demand.

6. FRAMEWORK & TIMING:
Total Duration: 60 Seconds.
Clip Count: 17 Clips.
Structure: High granularity to separate the "Good" flow (Gate 1) from the "System Failure" (Gates 2 & 3).

7. DIALOG-CHECK:
SYSTEM_ONLY. No spoken dialogue. Visual storytelling via HUD elements and environmental reaction.

8. SPATIAL CONTEXT (ARRIVAL):
COORD_JUMP_SNAP.
*   Visual: Henoch does not walk in. The camera frames an empty snowy expanse. A vertical slit of pixel-sorting distortion cuts the air. Henoch steps "out" of the slit, which snaps shut behind him like a CRT monitor powering down.
