CONCEPT: ORBITAL_CALIBRATION_PROTOCOL_V2

1. BIO-INJECTION (The Interface):
Method: "Optic Nerve Hijack".
Technology: We do not use external holograms. Uriel initiates a wireless handshake with Henoch's neural lace. The "vision" is injected directly into the optic nerve.
Physical Manifestation: Henoch’s irises dilate to 100%, turning the entire visible eye into a milky-white receiver surface (`OPTICAL_OVERRIDE_MODE`). He does not blink during the transmission.

2. MESH-BREAK (The Reveal):
Method: "Occlusion Culling & Wireframe Fallback".
Technology: To process the high-bandwidth data of the celestial mechanics, the system reduces the rendering quality of the surrounding environment. The "beautiful sky" texture strips away to reveal the low-poly wireframe sphere of the simulation dome. The clouds are replaced by visible vector wind-force lines.

3. PACING-STRATEGIE (The Rhythm):
Method: "Buffer Underrun Simulation".
Edit Pattern: The visual flow is not smooth. It mimics a CPU under heavy load. The movement of the celestial bodies "steps" in 15-degree increments. We use jump-cuts that skip exactly 4 frames to simulate a low refresh rate during the heavy data transfer, creating a jarring, mechanical stroboscopic effect.

4. AUDIO-DESIGN (The Soundscape):
Method: "Server Room Ambience".
Layer 1: A constant, deep 60Hz mains hum (ground loop noise).
Layer 2: The sound of high-pressure cooling fans spinning up when the Sun transfers light.
Layer 3: The "Voice" of Uriel is not audio but a resonant bone-conduction vibration.

5. TRANSITIONS (The Glitch):
Method: "I-Frame Destruction".
Visual: Transitions are not fades. They are datamoshes where the motion vectors of the previous clip drag the pixels of the next clip, smearing the image briefly before the keyframe snaps it back to clarity.

6. FRAMEWORK & TIMING:
Total Duration: ~70 Seconds.
Clip Count: 16 Clips.
Philosophy: "Show the Mechanism, not the Magic." Every movement must look powered by hydraulics or magnetism, not will.

7. SPATIAL CONTEXT (ARRIVAL):
Method: "Merkabah_Hover_State".
We are stationary at 30,000 feet. The Merkabah (a tetrahedron energy field) is in "Station Keeping" mode. The floor is transparent. We look down at the cloud layer, which acts as a projection screen for the shadows of the celestial gears.
