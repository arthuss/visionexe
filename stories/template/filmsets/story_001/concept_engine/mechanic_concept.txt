Method: I_FRAME_DESTRUCTION.
Technique: Scenes do not cut. The data of the current frame "breaks" into macro-blocking artifacts that resolve into the next scene. It looks like a corrupted video file correcting itself.

6. SPATIAL CONTEXT (ARRIVAL)
