### I. MECHANICS & TECHNICAL DEFINITIONS (REFINED)

**1. BIO-INJECTION (THE PROPHET'S INTERFACE)**
*   **Physical Manifestation**: `OPTICAL_BUS_OVERRIDE`. Enoch is not "seeing" a vision; he is projecting the Kernel Log. His biological eyes have been excised.
*   **Hardware**: `SOLAR_APERTURE_PROJECTORS` (Model: Seraphim-Optics) are seated in the bone orbits.
*   **Cooling**: The sheer data throughput generates massive heat. "Veins" are replaced with translucent tubing carrying liquid fluorocarbon coolant (Blue).
*   **Tears**: Coolant purge valve release to prevent retinal burnout during high-bandwidth transmission.

**2. MESH-BREAK DYNAMICS (GEOLOGICAL FAILURE)**
*   **The "Melting"**: `LOD_COLLAPSE` & `PHYSICS_ENGINE_ERROR`.
*   **Process**: The system cannot render the "God" entity and the environment simultaneously at full resolution. To save resources, the "World" (Mountains) is downgraded.
*   **Visuals**:
    *   **Phase 1**: Texture Decoupling. The "rock" bitmap slides off the wireframe.
    *   **Phase 2**: Voxelization. The geometry breaks into raw data blocks.
    *   **Phase 3**: State Change. Solid collision meshes are converted to fluid particles (`Viscosity: 100.0`) to prevent clipping errors.

**3. PACING & TEMPORAL DISTORTION**
*   **Variable Framerate**:
    *   **Kernel/God**: Uncapped (120fps+). Hyper-real, fluid.
    *   **Watchers/Daemons**: Throttled (12fps). Stop-motion, stuttering, "Lag" artifacts.
    *   **Enoch**: 24fps (The observer).

**4. AUDIO PROFILE**
*   **Ambience**: `SERVER_ROOM_ROAR`. The sound of a million cooling fans spinning up.
*   **The Voice**: 56k Modem Handshake amplified to 140dB. Digital screeching, not human speech.
*   **The Quake**: Sub-bass frequencies (5Hz - 20Hz) causing camera vibration, simulating structural stress.

**5. TRANSITIONS (INGRESS/EGRESS)**
*   **Method**: `NODE_HOPPING` (No flight).
*   **Visual**: `DATAMOSH/PIXEL_SORTING`. The current environment "tears" vertically. The pixels of the desert drag downwards, revealing the snow/mountain of the next node instantly.
