CONCEPT_CHAPTER_22_THE_ARCHIVE_OF_SOULS_V2

1. BIO-INJECTION
**Manifestation:** `RETINAL_LIDAR_PROJECTION`. Henoch’s biological eyes are overlayed with a `HEADS_UP_DISPLAY` (Amber/Monochrome). The environment is too dark ("dark to look at" - Seg 2) for standard optics. He projects a dynamic LIDAR grid (Laser Scan) onto the obsidian surfaces to perceive depth. His nervous system connects to the `WAVE_INTERFACE_BLOCK` (Tablet) via sub-dermal fiber cables in his fingertips, synchronizing his heartbeat with the server clock.

2. MESH-BREAK
**Geometry Failure:** `VOXEL_EXTRUSION`. The "Mountain" is initially a flat, perfect plane ("Very smooth" - Seg 2). As the system queries the data, the black surface erupts into dynamic voxel columns (Bar Charts), physically pushing out of the cliff face to represent storage volume. The "Hollow Places" are not natural caves but `RECESSED_SERVER_BAYS` that open mechanically like iris-shutters.

3. PACING-STRATEGIE
**Rhythm:** `SYSTEM_DIAGNOSTIC`. The pacing is methodical and analytical. Slow, stabilized pans (The Inspection). This clinical rhythm is disrupted by `ASYNCHRONOUS_GLITCHES` during the Abel sequence—simulating a corrupt video file (Macro-blocking, Datamoshing) to visualize the "murder" error code.

4. AUDIO-DESIGN
**Ambience:** `HVAC_TURBINES`. The "silence" of the desert is replaced by the massive, sub-bass thrum of industrial air conditioning required to cool the data center.
**Voices:** The "spirits" do not scream humanly. They emit `HIGH_FREQUENCY_TELEMETRY`—chirps, modulations, and static bursts. Raphael’s voice is isolated on a separate, clear audio channel (Admin Priority).

5. ÜBERGÄNGE
**Ingress:** `NODE_SWAP`. No walking. The previous scene (Chapter 21) tears vertically like burning film. The black obsidian geometry of Chapter 22 slides in from the sides to replace it. Instant transition from location A to B.
**Mode Switch:** The "Abel" segment transitions from 3D Geometry to `2D_WAVEFORM_ANALYSIS` on Henoch’s tablet, then back to 3D reality, blurring the line between the data representation and the physical manifestation.

6. SPATIAL CONTEXT (ARRIVAL)
**Method:** `COORDINATE_SHIFT`. Henoch stands still. The lighting environment snaps from "Day" to "Artificial Night".
**Establishing:** The Camera is tilted 90 degrees up. The "Mountain" is a vertical wall of servers extending infinitely upwards, referencing the "Great and High" description (Seg 1).

7. DIALOG-CHECK
**Status:** `ACTIVE`. Interaction with System Monitor Raphael.
**Technique:** `SHOT_REVERSE_SHOT`. Essential for the Q&A about the partitions.
1. Raphael OTS (Explaining).
2. Henoch Reverse (Processing).
3. Insert: The Holographic Data Model appearing between them.
