፡​ውእቱ፡​ዓቢይ፡​እስከ፡​አመ፡​ኵነኔ፡​ዓቢይ፡​በላዕሌሆሙ።**

Another long one.
*   `ወእሙንቱ፡​መካናት ... ገብሩ` (And those places they made...)
*   `ኀበ፡​ያነብርዎሙ` (where they might place them). Relative clause modifying `መካናት`.
*   `እስከ፡​ዕለተ፡​ኵነኔሆሙ` (until the day of their judgment). PP adjunct.
*   `ወእስከ፡​አመ፡​እድሜሆሙ` (and until the time of their appointed time). Coordinated PP adjunct.
*   `ዕድሜሆሙ፡​ወዕድሜ፡​ውእቱ፡​ዓቢይ` (their appointed time, and the time is great). This looks like a new clause.
*   `እስከ፡​አመ፡​ኵነኔ፡​ዓቢይ፡​በላዕሌሆሙ` (until the time of the great judgment upon them).
