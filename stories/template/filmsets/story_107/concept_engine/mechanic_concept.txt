TECHNICAL CONCEPT: BOOK OF ENOCH CHAPTER 107 - VERSION 2.0 (SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE)

1. BIO-INJECTION MECHANICS (OPTICAL DATA BURST)
Critique of V1.0: "Golden semi-solid light" is too mystical/fantasy-oriented.
Refined Mechanism V2.0: High-Bandwidth Optical Shunt.
Enoch does not project "light"; he projects raw binary data via a high-frequency Lidar array embedded in his ocular cavities. The transmission is a visible laser-grid scan (Green/Red spectrum) that maps Methuselah’s facial topography and injects the `GENETIC_HASH_KEY` directly into his optic nerve. Methuselah’s reaction is not awe, but a seizure-like "Write-Event" where his pupils dilate to 100% to accept the packet. The "Truth" is a checksum validation file (SHA-256 equivalent) confirming Noah’s biological integrity.

2. MESH-BREAK PHENOMENON (LOD COLLAPSE)
Critique of V1.0: "Jagged render-limit" was aesthetic but lacked causal logic.
Refined Mechanism V2.0: Buffer Overflow & Texture Streaming Latency.
The "Ends of the Earth" is a low-priority memory address. As Methuselah approaches Enoch, the environment fails to load high-res textures. Mountains are low-poly geometric primitives (pyramids/cones) without shaders. The skybox has visible UV-seams. The ground texture stretches infinitely into a "Hall of Mirrors" effect due to coordinate mapping errors. The "Secret" Enoch reveals is the wireframe underlying the simulation, visible because the rendering engine has crashed in this sector.

3. PACING STRATEGY
Phase 1 (The Glitch): Rapid, disorienting cuts (0.5s - 1.0s). Jump-cuts on axis to simulate frame-dropping due to high CPU load at the system edge.
Phase 2 (The Handshake): Extreme slow-motion (300fps) during the data transfer. The world freezes; only the data stream moves.
Phase 3 (The Restore): Upon seeing Noah, the frame rate stabilizes to a cinematic 24fps. Camera moves become fluid, utilizing steady-cam simulation to represent "System Stability."

4. AUDIO DESIGN
Critique of V1.0: "Sub-Bass Drone" is generic.
Refined Mechanism V2.0: Server Farm Ambience.
Background: The deafening roar of cooling fans (White Noise) and the high-pitched whine of coil whine (15kHz).
Voice: Enoch’s voice is not "flanged"; it is granularly synthesized, sounding like multiple audio tracks slightly out of sync (Packet Loss).
Effect: The "Naming" of Noah triggers a "BIOS Beep" (Post-Test Success) followed by the silence of a perfectly running drive.

5. TRANSITION LOGIC (NODE HOPPING)
Critique of V1.0: "Voxel-Dissolve" is too magical/cinematic.
Refined Mechanism V2.0: Coordinate Swap (Teleport).
Methuselah does not dissolve. He "clips" out of existence. One frame he is at the Edge (X:9999, Y:9999), the next frame he is at the Habitat (X:0050, Y:0020). There is no transition effect, only a hard cut accompanied by a "Texture Pop-in" sound (like a wet slap). This emphasizes the simulation nature; he is simply re-instanced.
