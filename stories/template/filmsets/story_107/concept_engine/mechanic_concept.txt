TECHNICAL CONCEPT: BOOK OF ENOCH CHAPTER 107 - VERSION 2.0 (SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE)

1. BIO-INJECTION MECHANICS (OPTICAL DATA BURST)
Mechanism: High-Bandwidth Optical Shunt via Lidar Array.
Physical Manifestation: Enoch does not speak with vocal cords. His faceplate/visage retracts to reveal a dual-aperture Lidar projector. He maps Methuselah’s retinal topography in real-time using a visible Green/Red laser grid. The "message" regarding Noah is a compressed binary packet (Hash Key) injected directly into Methuselah’s optic nerve at 400TB/s.
Biological Reaction: Methuselah experiences a "Write-Event seizure." His pupils dilate fully (blow out) to accommodate the bandwidth. Capillaries in the sclera burst, turning eyes red temporarily. He does not hear words; he hallucinates the raw code scrolling down his visual cortex.

2. MESH-BREAK PHENOMENON (RENDER LIMIT)
Mechanism: Coordinate Precision Loss (Far Lands Effect).
Visuals: At this distance from the System Core (Eden/Civilization), floating point errors occur. The terrain geometry vibrates (Z-fighting). Mountains are reduced to low-poly primitives (pyramids without displacement maps). Textures fail to stream, showing "Checkerboard" or "World Align" debug materials. The skybox has visible seams where the cubemap meets.
Physics: Movement is jerky (Rubber-banding). Methuselah moves at 15fps while Enoch (Admin) moves at 144fps, creating a jarring temporal disconnect.

3. PACING STRATEGY (LATENCY CURVE)
Phase 1 (The Lag): Stutter-step editing. Jump cuts on axis (0.2s) to simulate packet loss as Methuselah approaches the Admin Node.
Phase 2 (The Upload): Frozen Time. During the Lidar injection, the environment halts completely (0fps). Only the laser grid moves.
Phase 3 (The Patch): Cinematic Smoothness. Once the data is received, Methuselah’s local reality stabilizes. Camera motion becomes fluid (Steadicam) to represent the successful integration of the "Noah Protocol."

4. AUDIO DESIGN (SERVER FARM AMBIENCE)
Ambient Layer: No wind or nature sounds. A deafening, layered drone of industrial cooling fans (White Noise) and high-frequency coil whine (15kHz) representing high CPU load.
Voice: Enoch’s voice is granular synthesis. It sounds like three audio tracks layered with micro-latency (Chorus effect due to lag).
UI Sounds: Interaction triggers "Texture Pop-in" sounds (wet slaps) and "Data-Handshake" tones (Modem screech/DTMF tones) pitched down 4 octaves.

5. TRANSITION LOGIC (NODE HOPPING)
Method: Instantaneous Coordinate Re-allocation (Teleport).
Visual: Methuselah does not walk back to Lamech. He executes a `return()` function.
Effect: A Hard Cut. Frame A is the glitchy "Ends of the Earth" (Grey/Wireframe). Frame B is the hyper-detailed "Lamech’s Bunker" (Obsidian/Server Racks). No dissolve, no fade. Just a coordinate swap. The audio cuts instantly from "Fan Drone" to "Silence/Hum."

9. MULTI-SHOT GENERATION LOGIC (EXAMPLE: THE INJECTION)
To capture "Enoch injects the code," we require a sequence, not a single generation.
Prompt A: Enoch’s face transforming/opening Lidar array.
Prompt B: The laser beams traveling through space (Profile shot).
Prompt C: The beams impacting Methuselah’s eyes and his biological reaction.
Prompt D: The POV of the code scrolling.
This decomposition ensures the "Rule of Mechanism" is visible in every step.
