CONCEPT: SYSTEM KERNEL PANIC & RECOVERY (CHAPTER 83) - VERSION 2.0

1. BIO-INJECTION MECHANICS
**Manifestation:** "Thermal Throttling & Buffer Overflow".
Enoch is not just seeing a vision; he is processing a simulation too complex for his current kernel version (Student Build).
**Visuals:**
*   **Skin:** Translucent marble turns angry red around the port connections (Overheating).
*   **Venting:** High-pressure steam jets (Coolant purge) erupt from the cervical spine ports to prevent CPU melt.
*   **The "Word":** Not liquid, but "Sonic Cavitation". A visible distortion wave of compressed air and raw binary data (glowing glyphs) is forcibly ejected from his vocal emitter. It is a "System Beep" amplified to physical trauma levels.

2. MESH-BREAK PHYSICS
**The Event:** `RENDER_PIPELINE_FAILURE`.
*   **Skybox:** "Texture Atlas Unwrapping". The sky doesn't just fall; the UV coordinates detach. We see the flat 2D texture map of the stars curling up and sliding off the domed collision mesh, revealing the `NO_DRAW` magenta/black checkerboard behind it.
*   **Terrain:** "Vertex Explosion". The "mountains on mountains" is Z-fighting. Two landscape meshes occupy the same coordinates, flickering rapidly between Texture A and Texture B.
*   **The Abyss:** "Clipping Plane Error". The floor collider deactivates. Objects don't sink into water; they clip through the solid ground geometry, falling infinitely into the un-rendered grey void (`Y = -∞`).

3. PACING STRATEGY
*   **Phase 1 (Archive):** 24fps, Film Grain, Stable. (The Memory).
*   **Phase 2 (The Crash):** Variable Framerate (VFR). Drops to 12fps, then spikes to 120fps (Speed-ramping). "Tearing" artifacts (V-Sync off).
*   **Phase 3 (Admin Intervention):** Static, Orthographic framing. Symmetrical. Order imposed on chaos.
*   **Phase 4 (Reboot):** A "Scanline Sweep". A horizontal bar of white light travels top-to-bottom, rewriting the resolution from 144p back to 8K.

4. AUDIO DESIGN
*   **Ambience:** The drone of a 400Hz mains hum (Server Room).
*   **The Crash:** "Bit-Crushing". Audio sample rate drops from 48kHz to 8-bit. The sound of digital clipping (Square waves).
*   **Voices:** Mahalalel is not bassy; he is *Multi-Channel*. His voice sounds like it is coming from every speaker in the room (PA System override).

5. TRANSITIONS
*   **Ingress:** "LOD Pop-in". The detail levels snap instantly from low to high.
*   **Egress:** "Blue Screen Dump". The vision cuts instantly to a flat wall of hex code, then fades to black.

6. FRAMEWORK & TIMING
**Total Duration:** ~90 Seconds (Complex dialogue/coding sequence).
**Clip Count:** 18 Clips.
**Structure:**
*   Intro (Log): 2 Clips
*   The Crash (Simulation Failure): 5 Clips
*   The Intervention (Admin & Diagnostics): 6 Clips
*   The Patch (Coding & Reboot): 3 Clips
*   Outro (Log): 2 Clips

7. SPATIAL CONTEXT
**Location:** `SERVER_DORM_01` in the House of Mahalalel.
**Set Design:** Not a bedroom. A "Rack-Space". Stone slabs slide out of basalt walls like server blades.
