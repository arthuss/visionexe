CONCEPT: SYSTEM KERNEL PANIC & RECOVERY (CHAPTER 83)

1. BIO-INJECTION MECHANICS
Physical manifestation: "Heuristic Overflow". Enoch's body acts as a biological heat-sink for the crashing simulation.
Visuals: Sub-dermal cooling channels glow bright orange (thermal throttling). Steam vents violently from the ports on his neck and spine. His optical apertures don't just flicker; they project the "Blue Screen of Death" (fatal error hex codes) directly onto the ceiling.
The "Word fell into my mouth": A literal data-purge. Black "ferrofluid" liquid (corrupted data) bubbles up from his throat, forcing a physical purge/cough.

2. MESH-BREAK PHYSICS
The Vision is a "LOD (Level of Detail) Cascade Failure".
Sky Collapse: The Skybox texture map un-wraps. We see the 2D UV-grid peeling off the celestial sphere, revealing the raw static noise behind the simulation.
Terrain Failure: "Mountains on mountains": Polygon Decimation. High-poly terrain meshes instantly revert to low-poly blocks, then intersect violently (clipping), causing Z-fighting (flickering textures).
Object Deletion: Trees don't just fall; they are "De-rezzed". They turn into wireframe outlines before dissolving into binary dust. The "Abyss" is the `NULL_VOID` - the un-rendered grey grid at coordinate Y=-10000.

3. PACING STRATEGY
Phase 1 (The Log): Calm, archival framing.
Phase 2 (The Glitch): A "Datamosh" edit. Compression artifacts smear the motion. Audio skips. Frame rate drops to 12fps to simulate processing lag.
Phase 3 (Admin Mode): Stabilized camera, but lighting is "Emergency Red". High contrast. Sharp cuts.
Phase 4 (Reboot): A "Sweep" effect. A wave of white light restores high-res textures and anti-aliasing. Smooth 60fps returns.

4. AUDIO DESIGN
Ambience: The hum of a massive server farm, sudden silence (fan failure).
The Crash: "Digital Screaming" - synthesized audio tearing, bit-crushed distortion. The sound of a hard drive head scratching the platter.
Voices: Mahalalel sounds like deep bass resonance (Sub-woofer). Enoch sounds tinny and panicked (High-pass filter).

5. TRANSITIONS
Normal -> Vision: "Frame Freeze" followed by a pixel-sort melt.
Vision -> Normal: "Hard Reset". A sudden snap to black silence, then a loud gasp/intake of air.

6. FRAMEWORK & TIMING
Total Duration: ~75 Seconds (extended for dialogue pacing).
Clip Count: 18 Clips.
Structure: 3 Clips Intro (Current timeline) -> 5 Clips Crash (Flashback) -> 5 Clips Interaction (Dialogue) -> 3 Clips Restoration -> 2 Clips Outro.

7. DIALOGUE CHECK
Yes. Critical interaction between User (Enoch) and SysAdmin (Mahalalel).
Shot-Reverse-Shot required for the instruction to "Write the Prayer" (Code the Patch).

8. SPATIAL CONTEXT (ARRIVAL)
No travel. Enoch is docked in the `SLEEP_POD` within the `MAHALALEL_ARCHIVE_NODE`. The vision is internal, projected onto his retinal display.
