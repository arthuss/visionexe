[1. BIO-INJECTION MECHANICS]
Refinement: Removed "Magic Gold Nanites". Replaced with "Optical-Haptic Bridge".
- Interface: "Biometric Handshake". The obsidian terminal is a solid-state drive surface. Henou does not "sink" into it magically; the surface vibrates at a frequency that matches his bone density (Haptic Coupling).
- Manifestation: "Subsurface Scattering Overload". High-bandwidth data transfer generates heat. His veins glow white-hot (6500K) not due to "energy", but due to thermal exhaust from the data processing running through his neural lacing.
- Optics: "LIDAR Acquisition". His eyes do not just spin; they project a structured light grid (point cloud) onto the interface to map inputs.

[2. MESH-BREAK PHENOMENON]
Refinement: Removed "Voxels". Replaced with "Render Pipeline Failure".
- Geometry: "Culling Errors". The system stops rendering back-faces. We see through the floor geometry to the black void beneath. Vertices simply cease to exist (NaN coordinates), causing the mesh to stretch infinitely in a single frame (Artifacting).
- Texture: "UV-Map Collapse". Textures do not turn pink; they unwrap. We see the raw texture atlas (skin, stone, metal flat-maps) projected incorrectly onto 3D forms before they vanish.
- The "Fire": "Particle Entropy". The "Invisible Fire" is a particle system with missing collision data. It flows *through* objects, eroding their alpha channel (transparency) until they are deleted.

[3. PACING STRATEGY]
Rhythm: "Variable Refresh Rate (VRR)".
- The Observer (Henou): Renders at a locked, smooth 120fps. High fidelity.
- The Corrupt (Sinners): Render at 12fps. Stuttering, lagging. Their movement is jerky and misses keyframes, visually distinguishing their low-priority status.
- The Event (Purge): "Frame Tearing". The edits are not clean cuts; they are hard glitches where the top half of the frame lags behind the bottom half.

[4. AUDIO DESIGN]
Refinement: Removed "Screaming". Replaced with "Digital Distortion".
- Ambience: "Server Room White Noise". A constant, oppressive 60Hz hum of cooling arrays.
- The "Crying": "Coil Whine". The high-pitched electromagnetic vibration of capacitors under extreme load. Combined with "Bit-Crushing" (audio resolution dropping to 4-bit).
- The "Angel": "Sub-Harmonics". Not a voice, but a low-frequency pulse (20Hz) that vibrates the camera lens, causing visual blur during speech.

[5. TRANSITIONS]
- Ingress: "LOD Pop-In". Objects do not fade in. They snap from Low Poly (blocky) to High Poly (detailed) instantly as the camera gets closer.
- State Change: "Buffer Clear". The screen flashes absolute black for 1 frame (Empty Buffer) between major sequence shifts.
