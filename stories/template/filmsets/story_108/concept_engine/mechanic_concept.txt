[1. BIO-INJECTION MECHANICS]
Subject Henou (Enoch) operates in "Write-Privilege Mode".
- Interface: Direct Neural-Link via haptic touch. Fingertips do not just touch the surface; they merge with the obsidian glass, liquefying into gold data-streams that pulse into the hardware.
- Feedback: Subdermal bioluminescence. Gold circuitry patterns ignite under the skin of his forearms, syncing with the data-write speed.
- Optics: Iris mechanics shift to "Record Mode". Pupils dilate fully, replaced by a rotating optical shutter mechanism capturing the audit log.

[2. MESH-BREAK PHENOMENON]
The "End of Earth" is a rendering limit event.
- Geometry: The environment is not burning; it is culling. High-poly meshes degrade to Low-poly, then to Wireframe, then to Null.
- The "Fire": A vertical column of raw, uncompiled pixel-noise. It is not combustion; it is a rendering error where coordinate values exceed the engine's maximum float precision, resulting in a vibrating column of pure white/cyan static.
- The "Cloud": A wall of dense volumetric fog representing the "Unrendered Area" or "Fog of War" at the edge of the map.

[3. PACING STRATEGY]
Rhythm: "System Purge".
- Phase 1 (Audit): Slow, methodical camera movements. Stable tracking shots. 60 BPM.
- Phase 2 (Glitch): Rapid, jarring cuts during the "Sinner" visualization. Datamoshing transitions where frames bleed into each other.
- Phase 3 (Optimization): Smooth, floating camera motion for the "Righteous" sequence. High frame-rate feel (120fps simulation).

[4. AUDIO DESIGN]
Industrial/Server Soundscape.
- Ambience: A constant, low-frequency thrum (40Hz) of a massive cooling system.
- The "Crying": High-pitched digital screeching, similar to dial-up handshake failures or corrupted audio files played at 10x speed.
- The "Voice": The Angel does not speak with vocal cords. The voice is a resonant sub-bass frequency that vibrates the camera lens, overlaid with teletype data-chatter.

[5. TRANSITIONS]
- Ingress: "Noclip". The camera passes through solid geometry to reveal the void behind the texture.
- State Change: "Voxel-Dissolve". Entities do not fade; they break apart into constituent cubic voxels which either float up (Upload) or turn black and scatter (Delete).
