- Optics: Eyes lock into "Terminal Mode". Sclera turns black; iris becomes a rapidly spinning loading ring (white-gold).

[2. MESH-BREAK PHENOMENON]

- Geometry: "Vertex Explosion". The floor meshes near the edge are not solid; vertices are being pulled into infinity (0,0,0 coordinate reset).
- Texture: "Missing_Texture_Pink". Occasional frames flash the checkerboard default texture before dissolving into wireframe.
- The "Fire": A "Voxel-Avalanche". It is a cascade of unassigned pixels falling into a memory leak.
